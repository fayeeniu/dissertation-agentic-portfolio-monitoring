    \textbf{Local runtime and accountable actor}\par NFR-SEC-001; FR-UI-003--004 &
    One configured local operator uses the FastAPI/Jinja interface, SQLite and ignored local files. &
    CLI loopback bind; Host/client allowlists; CSRF on mutations; configured reviewer identity; Docker clients accepted only behind a host-loopback published port. &
    Formal accessibility/security review and any wider deployment design require separate evidence and authority. &
    No anonymous, remote or multi-user access. Reject non-loopback clients, unexpected hosts, missing CSRF or missing configured reviewer. &
    \emph{cli.py}; \emph{web.py}; route tests.\par \textbf{IN PLACE}\par Local only; not production auth. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Identity-first intake}\par FR-ID-001--004; FR-INT-001, 006 &
    A submitted Companies House number is an identity claim; a named reviewer may accept or reject it with rationale. &
    Structurally valid number-only intake creates an unresolved company and unreviewed identifier. Names/domains do not auto-merge; identity conflict and classification mismatch fail closed. &
    Before direct live registry use: close G2 with current interface/terms, credential, identifier, storage, cutoff and attribution review. &
    Syntax is not legal-identity authority. Do not collect or link facts while identity is unresolved, conflicting, rejected or from another classification boundary. &
    Intake service; ADR-0007; tests.\par \textbf{IN PLACE}\par Offline; live registry held. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Data class and authority}\par NFR-SEC-002--005; NFR-RES-001 &
    Restricted and internal material stays local; admitted public evidence and fictional synthetic cases follow separate flows. &
    Closed classification enum; purpose and actor records; ignored create-once files with mode ``0600''; content-free traces; provider recheck. &
    Verify lawful authority, controller, access, retention and deletion, and any ethics/consent terms before restricted-data or participant work. &
    Never place restricted or internal content in Git, fixtures, raw logs or an external model. Unclear authority or unexpected personal data stops processing. &
    \emph{enums.py}; \emph{security.py}; boundary tests.\par \textbf{CONTROL IMPLEMENTED}; authority not verified. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Public and model boundary}\par FR-EXT-004--008; FR-RES-001--004 &
    Only a public case with reviewed company number and cutoff may enter bounded URL discovery, capture and optional extraction; synthetic fixtures rehearse the path offline. &
    External and retrieval gates default closed; snippets/prose are candidates, not evidence; guarded HTTPS capture, injection rejection, ``store=False'', strict span/schema checks and independent verification remain mandatory. &
    Separately authorise and evaluate the live public company path; finish source-specific G2 review and verify provider controls at execution. Direct registry execution remains P1. &
    No restricted or internal model input, person profiling, arbitrary browsing, snippet support, autonomous recommendation or model approval/export. Missing, held or conflicting evidence cannot become support. &
    \emph{bootstrap.py}; ADR-0008; fake boundary tests.\par \textbf{CODE/LOCAL TEST EVIDENCE}; live company path unrun. \\
    \pagebreak

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Review and export}\par FR-VER-002; FR-REP-004--009 &
    The configured reviewer assesses a versioned report or profile with verification records, then approves, rejects or returns it. &
    Actor, reason and version audit; optimistic lock; edits revoke approval; no export before approval; manifest-backed files and hash checks on reuse. &
    Authorised C0/C3 observation and reviewer utility remain pending; production roles or signed approval need a separately scoped design. &
    No model or workflow stage can approve or publish. Unverified claims, stale versions, rejected reports, missing approval, tampering or incomplete writes stop export. &
    \emph{reporting.py}; approval and export tests.\par \textbf{IN PLACE}\par Offline; human result unavailable. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Prototype exclusions}\par Charter P0/P1/P2 &
    Engineering evaluation of bounded ingestion, evidence, verification and local review for RQ1--RQ3. &
    Fixed stage contracts, explicit failures, provenance and local audit make unsupported actions visible rather than silently expanding scope. &
    Live connectors, participants, restricted-data study or deployment may proceed only through named future gates; they are not completion criteria for this slice. &
    No supplied credential use, production tenancy, social scraping, unrestricted crawling, autonomous publication, investment decisions, fine-tuning, cloud deployment or native PPTX/PDF generation. &
    Project Charter ``Explicit non-goals''; requirements P1/P2; architecture gaps.\par \textbf{CONTRACT BOUNDARY}; not an empirical result. \\
