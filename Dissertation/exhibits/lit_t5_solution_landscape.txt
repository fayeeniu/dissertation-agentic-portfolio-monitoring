LIT-T5 - Alternative portfolio-reporting approaches and unresolved evidence
Target section: 2.7 Main findings from the literature and research gap
LaTeX label: tab:lit-solution-landscape

Purpose

The matrix compares each material alternative against the same criteria: its primary workflow and
inputs, its documented evidence or control position, and the evidence that remains unresolved for this
study. It is a literature and vendor-documentation synthesis, not a score, benchmark result or product
recommendation.

Interpretation boundary

Manual work, deterministic processing, a single grounded process, RAG, bounded independent
verification and named human review are distinct methodological alternatives. The verifier row maps
to RQ2; the human-review row maps to RQ3. Visible, Zapflow and Affinity are named only to establish the current
commercial categories described on their official pages. Their capabilities are vendor-stated; the
captured pages do not independently establish accuracy, completeness, time savings, reviewer benefit,
UK early-stage suitability or superiority. Unknown properties remain unknown.
