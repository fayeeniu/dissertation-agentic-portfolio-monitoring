    \textbf{Restricted, personal or secret data leaves its authority boundary} &
    Classify at intake and again at the provider boundary. Keep restricted and internal material local and ignored; use mode ``0600'' snapshot and export files; trace IDs, counts and hashes rather than raw values; forbid external processing. &
    Classification, purpose and actor; snapshot and export checksum and manifest; review decision; content-free trace; security-gate record. &
    Unclear authority, unexpected personal data, an exposed credential or an unapproved processor: stop collection, isolate and record the event, then seek supervisor, ethics or security direction. &
    \textbf{CONTROL IMPLEMENTED / TESTED.}\par Real-data authority, retention and deletion schedule, and credential-rotation confirmation are \textbf{NOT VERIFIED}; no restricted external execution is claimed. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Unauthorised C0 or C3 observation or reviewer feedback} &
    Require recorded ethics/data authority, consent and withdrawal, approved participant information and instrument, pseudonymised IDs, data minimisation and predeclared quotation use. &
    Approval or waiver; dated information and consent versions; separate ID linkage; task, review and withdrawal records; pre- and post-review version hashes. &
    Missing or unclear approval, consent or withdrawal handling: do not recruit, observe or collect; retain the outcome as null/unavailable and escalate. &
    \textbf{APPROVAL EVIDENCE NOT VERIFIED / NO-GO.}\par Human C0 and C3 execution and qualitative feedback are unavailable; no participant result is claimed. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{D0, D1 or D2 leakage and holdout contamination} &
    Admit D0 by hash and namespace; admit D1 only after authority; keep D2 pathless and sealed until the evaluation bundle freezes; preserve group and period separation; make any D2-informed change exploratory. &
    Dataset manifest, hash and source version; group and period split; code, rule, prompt, model and analysis versions; loader outcome; access and deviation log. &
    Broken seal, checksum or group failure, D2 inspection or post-access tuning: halt confirmatory work, record the deviation and seek supervisor review; any resumed run is exploratory. &
    \textbf{D0 CONTROL IMPLEMENTED / TESTED.}\par D1 is protocol-only and unavailable. D2 is sealed and loader-blocked. No D1 or D2 result is claimed. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Unlawful, unavailable or after-cutoff public-source use} &
    Use versioned source admission, reviewed identity and cutoff, bounded GET-only access, robots and access checks, and no form, login, paywall or CAPTCHA bypass; review licence and redistribution per source. &
    Capability and terms state with review date; request fingerprint; availability, retrieval and cutoff times; capture hash and locator; inaccessible or held state. &
    Unknown or changed terms, forbidden access, after-cutoff support or redistribution uncertainty: hold the source or claim; do not fetch or export; require named review. &
    \textbf{BOUNDED CONTROLS IMPLEMENTED.}\par Local public-web use is conditional; direct registry evaluation and redistribution remain held or unavailable. \\
    \pagebreak

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Prompt injection or unauthorised model use} &
    Treat retrieved text as data, reject instruction-like content, allow opt-in public or synthetic input only, set ``store=False'', require strict schema and exact span, bound models and attempts, and deny approval or export authority. &
    Classification; provider, model and schema versions; attempt, token and error telemetry; exact span and verification; rejection or abstention reason; content-free manifest. &
    Injection, personal or restricted content, arbitrary routing, unclear processor authority or failed span validation: reject or abstain before claim support; stop model use and export. &
    \textbf{CONTROL IMPLEMENTED / TESTED.}\par No D1, D2 or participant processing is claimed; no model quality, provider retention or superiority result is asserted. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Version or configuration drift; incomplete audit} &
    Pin revision and diff, dependencies, migration, manifests and cutoff, catalogue, codebook, prompts, schemas, connectors and models; remove secrets; retain run and agent IDs and analysis and exhibit hashes. &
    Reproducibility manifest; immutable input and result hashes; environment and migration record; attempts, failures and human decisions; deviation log; generated-artifact hashes. &
    Missing or mismatched hash or version, secret in the bundle, unexplained deviation or non-repeat: fail or hold the comparison, preserve the failure and resolve before making a claim. &
    \textbf{MECHANISMS IMPLEMENTED / TESTED.}\par D0 repeat evidence exists; a final empirical run bundle is unavailable because held conditions have not executed. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Reference bias; builder and evaluator bias; selective reporting} &
    Plan a versioned codebook, two authorised independent labels and adjudication; blind scoring where practicable; predefine outcomes and denominators; retain exclusions, failures, null and negative results and narrow conclusions. &
    Separate labels; agreement and adjudication and codebook versions; reference and scorer hash; eligibility flow; analysis and deviation log; limitations. &
    No authorised frozen reference, outcome-driven rule change, unavailable denominator or unresolved disagreement: no confirmatory score; label exploratory or held and seek supervisor and statistical review. &
    \textbf{DOCUMENTED PLAN.}\par Gold reference and final protocol are not frozen; C0, C3, D1 or D2 empirical or inferential execution is unavailable. \\
