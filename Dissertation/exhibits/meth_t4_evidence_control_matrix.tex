    \textbf{Restricted, personal or secret data is sent without permission} &
    Classify data at intake and again before an external provider. Keep restricted and internal material local and out of external processing. Use private file permissions and record IDs, counts and hashes rather than raw values. &
    Data class, purpose and person responsible; source-copy and export checksum and file list; review decision; record without content; security check. &
    Unclear authority, unexpected personal data, an exposed credential or an unapproved processor: stop collection, isolate and record the event, then seek supervisor, ethics or security direction. &
    \textbf{CONTROL BUILT / TESTED.}\par Permission for real data, retention and deletion times, and confirmation that exposed keys were changed are \textbf{NOT VERIFIED}. No restricted external run is claimed. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Unauthorised C0 or C3 observation or reviewer feedback} &
    Require recorded ethics and data permission, consent and withdrawal rules, approved participant information and questionnaire, coded IDs, minimum data and a quotation plan set in advance. &
    Approval or waiver; dated information and consent versions; separate ID link; task, review and withdrawal records; hashes before and after review. &
    If approval, consent or withdrawal handling is missing or unclear, do not recruit, observe or collect. Keep the result blank and ask for guidance. &
    \textbf{APPROVAL NOT VERIFIED / ON HOLD.}\par Human C0 and C3 work and feedback are unavailable. No participant result is claimed. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{D0, D1 or D2 information leaks across study stages} &
    Accept D0 by hash and test name. Use D1 only after permission. Keep D2 sealed with no visible path until the evaluation choices are fixed. Keep company and period groups separate. A change based on D2 makes the run exploratory. &
    Dataset list, hash and source version; group and period split; code, rule, prompt, model and analysis versions; loader result; access log and changes from the plan. &
    If the seal, checksum or group split fails, or D2 is viewed or used for tuning, stop the final study. Record the change and ask the supervisor to review it. Any later run is exploratory. &
    \textbf{D0 CONTROL BUILT / TESTED.}\par D1 has not run. D2 is sealed and blocked by the loader. No D1 or D2 result is claimed. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Public source is used unlawfully, is unavailable or is too late} &
    Use versioned source rules, reviewed identity and cut-off, GET-only access with clear limits, robots and access checks. Do not bypass forms, sign-in, paywalls or CAPTCHAs. Review licence and sharing rules for each source. &
    Source ability and terms with review date; request fingerprint; availability, collection and cut-off times; copy hash and exact location; inaccessible or held state. &
    Unknown or changed terms, forbidden access, after-cutoff support or redistribution uncertainty: hold the source or claim; do not fetch or export; require named review. &
    \textbf{LIMITED CONTROLS BUILT.}\par Local public-web use is conditional. Direct live registry evaluation and sharing remain on hold or unavailable. \\
    \pagebreak

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Prompt injection or unauthorised model use} &
    Treat downloaded text as data and reject content that looks like instructions. Allow only chosen public or synthetic input, set ``store=False'', require a strict schema and exact passage, limit models and attempts, and never give the model approval or export rights. &
    Data class; provider, model and schema versions; attempt, token and error logs; exact passage and verification; reason for rejection or no claim; file list without content. &
    Injection, personal or restricted content, arbitrary routing, unclear processor authority or failed span validation: reject or abstain before claim support; stop model use and export. &
    \textbf{CONTROL BUILT / TESTED.}\par D1, D2 and participant processing have not run. There is no result for model quality, provider retention or better performance. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Version or setting changes; incomplete record} &
    Fix the code revision and changes, dependencies, database version, source and dataset lists, cut-off, catalogue, labelling guide, prompts, schemas, connectors and models. Remove secrets. Keep run and agent IDs and hashes for analysis and exhibits. &
    Repeatability record; write-once input and result hashes; environment and database version; attempts, failures and human decisions; changes from the plan; hashes for generated files. &
    A missing or mismatched hash or version, secret in the set, unexplained change or failed repeat stops the comparison. Keep the failure and resolve it before making a claim. &
    \textbf{MECHANISMS BUILT / TESTED.}\par D0 repeat evidence exists. There is no final study record because the held conditions have not run. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Reference bias; builder and evaluator bias; selective reporting} &
    Plan a versioned labelling guide, two authorised people working independently and a recorded final decision. Keep scoring blind where practical. Set outcomes and calculation totals in advance. Keep exclusions, failures, blank and negative results, and limit conclusions. &
    Separate labels; agreement, final decisions and labelling-guide versions; reference and scorer hash; case flow; analysis, changes from the plan and limits. &
    No authorised fixed reference, a rule changed after seeing outcomes, an unavailable calculation total or an unresolved disagreement means no final score. Mark the run exploratory or held and seek supervisor and statistical review. &
    \textbf{DOCUMENTED PLAN.}\par Reference answers and the final study plan are not fixed. C0, C3, D1 and D2 have no study results. \\
