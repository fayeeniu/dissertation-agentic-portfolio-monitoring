Primary question

How it will be answered: Combine the findings for RQ1 to RQ3 across C0 manual work, C1 and C2 automation, and C3 named review.

Main measures: Amount automated; quality; reliability; complete source records; failures; and active and elapsed time.

Current evidence and limit: D0 tests C1 and C2 mechanisms only. C0, C3, D1 and D2 are on hold, so the primary question is partly answered.

RQ1 - input-to-claim accuracy and reliability

How it will be answered: Apply fixed identity, date, missing-data and source rules to labelled cases, then compare the output with agreed reference answers.

Main measures: Identity, value, period and missing-state accuracy; source completeness; and repeated-run agreement.

Current evidence and limit: D0 provides labelled mechanism evidence. D1 and sealed D2 are held; no real-portfolio accuracy is established.

RQ2 - effect of independent verification

How it will be answered: Compare C1 without a separate verifier with C2 on the same cases and with the same information access.

Main measures: Precision; recall; unsupported-claim rate; conflicts; rejected claims; source completeness; and repeatability.

Current evidence and limit: C1 and C2 have been compared on D0. D1 and D2 are held; the result does not establish performance on new company data.

RQ3 - manual reporting and named human review

How it will be answered: Compare manual C0, automated C1 and C2, and named-review C3 on the same tasks, sources and reference answers.

Main measures: Correctness; completeness; active and elapsed time; edits; approval time; and reviewer usefulness.

Current evidence and limit: No C0 or C3 observations exist. The authorised human comparison is on hold, so RQ3 is unanswered.

Contract-wide interpretive boundary

Live public-web company-research evaluation is outside RQ1 to RQ3. The table does not evaluate investment outcomes and does not establish production readiness or complete web coverage. It adds no study result.
