METH-T4 - Ethics, leakage and reproducibility evidence-control matrix
Target section: 3.7 Ethics, leakage prevention, and reproducibility
LaTeX label: tab:meth-evidence-control-matrix

Purpose and evidence boundary
This is Faye Niu's conceptual, non-empirical synthesis of the current draft evaluation protocol,
governance rules and implemented repository controls. It maps each material risk or authority
boundary to a preventive control, the record that must be retained, a stop or escalation condition,
and the current evidence state. It does not assert ethics approval, participant recruitment or
consent, restricted-data authority, D1 or D2 access, final protocol execution, live evaluation,
production security, legal compliance, model quality, human benefit or an empirical result.

Status key
- IMPLEMENTED / TESTED: repository code exists and the selected local checks listed below exercise
  the stated mechanism. This does not prove control effectiveness in every deployment or dataset.
- DOCUMENTED PLAN: the protocol defines a future control or record, but the relevant execution is
  not available and no outcome may be inferred.
- APPROVAL EVIDENCE NOT VERIFIED: the repository does not contain sufficient local evidence to
  claim that approval was granted or refused. This is a no-go state, not a claim about an external
  ethics decision.
- UNAVAILABLE / SEALED: the condition or data cannot supply a result. Null and unavailable states
  must not be replaced with synthetic measurements or zero.

Linear reading order

1. Restricted, personal or secret data leaves its authority boundary

Risk or boundary
Restricted or internal portfolio data, unexpected personal data, a credential, or another secret
could enter Git, raw logs, an export or an external processor without appropriate authority.

Preventive control
Classify material at intake and again at the external-provider boundary. Keep restricted and
internal material local, purpose-limited and ignored by version control. Snapshot and export files
use mode 0600. Agent traces retain IDs, counts, timings and hashes rather than raw restricted
values. Restricted and internal content is forbidden from external-model processing.

Retained evidence
Retain classification, purpose and actor; snapshot and export checksums and manifests; the named
review decision; content-free trace records; and the security-gate record. Hashes provide integrity
evidence, not encryption or anonymisation.

Stop or escalation
If authority is unclear, unexpected personal data appears, a credential remains exposed or content
would cross an unapproved processor, stop collection. Isolate and record the event without copying
the sensitive value, then seek supervisor, ethics or security direction.

Current status
CONTROL IMPLEMENTED / TESTED for the local classification, file-mode, trace and external-provider
mechanisms. Real-data authority, the retention and deletion schedule, and confirmation that the
known credential was rotated are NOT VERIFIED. No restricted external execution is claimed.

2. Unauthorised C0 or C3 observation or reviewer feedback

Risk or boundary
Manual-baseline observation, usability work or qualitative reviewer feedback could be collected
without a recorded ethics/data basis, consent, withdrawal arrangements or an approved instrument.

Preventive control
Require recorded ethics and data authority, participant information, consent and withdrawal,
pseudonymised participant identifiers with a separately stored linkage, data minimisation, an
approved task/instrument, and a predeclared rule for any direct quotation.

Retained evidence
Retain the approval or waiver record; dated participant-information and consent versions; the
separately controlled ID linkage; task, review and withdrawal records; and pre- and post-review
report-version hashes.

Stop or escalation
If approval, consent or withdrawal handling is missing or unclear, do not recruit, observe or
collect. Preserve the outcome as null or unavailable and seek supervisor/ethics direction.

Current status
APPROVAL EVIDENCE NOT VERIFIED / NO-GO. Human C0 and C3 execution and consented qualitative
feedback are unavailable. No participant, timing, usability, edit-burden or thematic result is
claimed.

3. D0, D1 or D2 leakage and holdout contamination

Risk or boundary
Visible synthetic data, pilot evidence or the final out-of-sample holdout could shape a purportedly
confirmatory method or cross entity/period partitions.

Preventive control
Admit D0 through its versioned manifest, checksum and benchmark namespace. Admit D1 only after
licence, provenance and authority gates are approved. Keep D2 pathless and sealed until the full
evaluation bundle is frozen. Preserve group- and period-aware separation. Treat any D2-informed
post-access change as exploratory rather than silently confirmatory.

Retained evidence
Retain dataset manifest, checksum, source version and namespace; group/period split; code, rule,
prompt, schema, model and analysis versions; loader outcome; and access/deviation log.

Stop or escalation
A broken D2 seal, manifest checksum or grouping failure, D2 inspection, or post-access tuning stops
confirmatory work. Record the deviation and seek supervisor review. Any authorised resumed run is
exploratory unless a transparent protocol amendment establishes a valid new design.

Current status
D0 admission, checksum, namespace and loader mechanisms are IMPLEMENTED / TESTED. D1 remains
protocol-only with no admitted path or checksum. D2 remains pathless, sealed and loader-blocked.
No D1 or D2 run or result is claimed.

4. Unlawful, unavailable or after-cutoff public-source use

Risk or boundary
An implemented connector could be mistaken for permission to retrieve, store or redistribute a
source, or later-published evidence could support an earlier reporting cutoff.

Preventive control
Require versioned source admission, reviewed identity and cutoff, bounded GET-only access,
robots/access checks, no form, authentication, paywall or CAPTCHA bypass, and source-specific
licence, terms, retention, attribution and redistribution review.

Retained evidence
Retain capability and terms state with dated review, exact request fingerprint, source availability,
retrieval and cutoff times, immutable capture checksum and locator, and inaccessible or held state.

Stop or escalation
Unknown or changed terms, forbidden access, after-cutoff support or redistribution uncertainty
holds the source and associated claim. Do not fetch or export; require a named admission review.

Current status
Bounded local source-admission and public-web controls are implemented. Public-web use is
conditional and source-specific; implementation is not a general publisher licence. Direct
registry evaluation and public redistribution remain held or unavailable. No live evaluation
result or exhaustive-web claim is made.

5. Prompt injection or unauthorised model use

Risk or boundary
Retrieved content could be mistaken for an instruction, restricted or personal content could cross
the external-provider boundary, or a model output could gain approval/export authority.

Preventive control
Treat retrieved content as untrusted data and reject instruction-like text before extraction or
model processing. External processing is opt-in and restricted to eligible public or synthetic
evidence. Set store=False, require a strict schema and literal exact span, enforce the model
allowlist and bounded attempts, independently normalise/verify output, and deny the model any
approval or export authority.

Retained evidence
Retain classification; provider, model and schema versions; attempt, token and error telemetry;
exact span and verification record; rejection or abstention reason; and content-free execution
manifest. Unknown cost or unavailable provider-control evidence remains null rather than estimated.

Stop or escalation
Injection, personal or restricted content, arbitrary model routing, unclear processor authority or
failed span validation causes rejection or abstention before claim support. Stop model use and
prevent export.

Current status
The classification, injection, provider-policy and report-authority mechanisms are IMPLEMENTED /
TESTED. No D1, D2 or participant processing is claimed. No model-quality, provider-retention,
completeness, cost or superiority result is asserted.

6. Version or configuration drift; incomplete audit

Risk or boundary
An evaluation could be impossible to reproduce or interpret because code, dependencies, data,
prompts, schemas, models, cutoffs, analysis or deviations are missing or have changed.

Preventive control
Pin the Git revision and diff, exact dependencies, migration revision, dataset and source manifests,
availability cutoff, catalogue, codebook, prompts, schemas, connectors and models. Remove secrets
from retained configuration. Retain stable run and agent IDs and analysis/table/figure hashes.

Retained evidence
Retain a reproducibility manifest; immutable input and result hashes; environment and migration
record; attempts, timings, failures and authorised human decisions; a deviation log; scoring and
analysis code; and hashes for generated files.

Stop or escalation
A missing or mismatched hash or version, secret in the bundle, unexplained deviation or failed
repeat holds the comparison. Preserve the failure and resolve it before making a comparative claim.

Current status
Repository observability, dependency/migration controls and D0 repeat mechanisms are IMPLEMENTED /
TESTED. A final empirical run bundle is unavailable because held D1, D2, C0 and human C3 execution
has not occurred.

7. Reference bias, builder/evaluator bias and selective reporting

Risk or boundary
Gold labels may reflect judgement, the student builds and evaluates the artefact, and favourable
outcomes could be selected while failures, nulls or negative cases are hidden.

Preventive control
Plan a versioned codebook, two authorised independent labels and reasoned adjudication. Blind
condition scoring where practicable. Predefine outcomes, denominators and deviation handling.
Retain exclusions, failures, null/negative results and unexpected regressions, and keep conclusions
within the executed population and protocol.

Retained evidence
Retain separate labels, agreement and adjudication records, codebook versions, frozen reference and
scorer hashes, eligibility/exclusion/failure flow, analysis and deviation logs, negative cases and
explicit limitations.

Stop or escalation
Without an authorised frozen reference, with an outcome-driven rule change, unavailable denominator
or unresolved disagreement, do not report a confirmatory score. Label the work exploratory or held
and seek supervisor and statistical review.

Current status
DOCUMENTED PLAN. The gold reference and final protocol are not constructed/frozen. C0, C3, D1 and
D2 empirical or inferential execution remains unavailable. No comparative or human result is
asserted.

Local literature basis
- nist2023airmf: Dissertation/sources/papers/11_nist_ai_rmf_1_0.pdf, PDF pages 32-35.
  Supports documented scope, human oversight, human-subject protections, rigorous testing,
  uncertainty, privacy and independent assessment. This voluntary framework does not certify the
  artefact or prove that these controls are effective.
- gebru2021datasheets: Dissertation/sources/papers/20_gebru_datasheets_for_datasets.pdf, PDF pages
  2 and 4-10. Supports lifecycle documentation covering purpose, composition, collection,
  consent/privacy, external-resource restrictions, licence/terms, distribution and maintenance.
  It does not establish this project's authority or source rights.
- greshake2023indirect: Dissertation/sources/papers/29_greshake_indirect_prompt_injection.pdf, PDF
  pages 1 and 3-5. Supports treating retrieved content as an indirect prompt-injection boundary.
  Demonstrated attacks do not establish completeness of this project's detector or security.
- kapoor2023leakage: Dissertation/sources/papers/32_kapoor_data_leakage.pdf, PDF pages 1-7 and
  17-21. Supports explicit leakage controls, dependence-aware partitioning and reproducibility
  reporting. It does not prove that this study has executed a leakage-free final evaluation.
- pineau2021reproducibility: Dissertation/sources/papers/33_pineau_reproducibility.pdf, PDF pages
  1-5 and 9-13. Supports recording code, data, procedures, metrics, statistics and reporting detail
  needed to assess reproducibility. Reproducibility is not equivalent to validity or generalisation.

Validation and limitations
The LaTeX source is designed as a two-page portrait longtable with repeated text headers and an
explicit page break after the fourth control family. Meaning does not rely on colour. This file is
the complete linear reading-order alternative. The exhibit is a control/status map, not evidence
that ethics approval, legal permission, consent, security, privacy, reproducibility, absence of
leakage, human utility or empirical effectiveness has been achieved. Implemented controls remain
prototype mechanisms; documented plans, unavailable conditions and unverified approvals retain
their separate evidence states.
