METH-T2 - Gold-reference construction and source-access parity controls
Target section: 3.4 Gold/reference construction and source parity
LaTeX label: tab:meth-reference-parity-matrix

Purpose and current status
This is Faye Niu's conceptual, non-empirical synthesis of the current project evaluation contract.
It defines how a reference should be constructed and how comparable workflow conditions should
receive equivalent eligible information. The gold/reference has not yet been constructed or
frozen. Therefore no empirical score, sample size, agreement value, acceptance threshold,
condition winner or human/system performance claim is asserted.

Reading-order alternative

Stage 1 - Define a versioned codebook and source policy
Create a codebook from the project data dictionary with worked metric, claim, source and reporting-
period examples. Define the label schema, eligible-source rules and source availability cutoff.
Record the codebook version and hash, source-policy version and eligible-source-manifest template.
Do not begin labelling or scoring while scope, definitions, examples, source eligibility or cutoff
remain ambiguous.

Stage 2 - Obtain two independent authorised label records
Give two authorised domain reviewers exactly the same frozen source bundle and instructions. Each
reviewer labels independently, with separate typed records retained until agreement is calculated.
Record pseudonymous reviewer identifiers, bundle and instruction versions, label timestamps and
authority state. Hold the process if a reviewer is unauthorised, bundles differ or reviewers reach
consensus during the independent pass. Where two reviewers are not ethically or operationally
available, preserve that limitation rather than fabricating independence.

Stage 3 - Capture the complete reference fields
For every in-scope company-period field or claim, record:
- canonical value or explicit missing state;
- evidence eligibility;
- sourceability class;
- reporting/value period;
- claim-support decision; and
- required report section.

Link each non-missing decision to the frozen source and exact locator. Retain missing, ambiguous,
inaccessible and conflicting states. A fluent statement, visible citation or prior report does not
establish support by itself.

Stage 4 - Measure agreement before adjudication
Report raw observed agreement with its exact denominator and label task. Add a task-appropriate
chance-corrected statistic only when the sample, item structure, number of reviewers, label scale,
missingness and design assumptions support that statistic. Record the chosen coefficient,
assumptions and uncertainty. If those conditions are not met, record the chance-corrected result as
null and explain why. Do not import a conventional acceptance threshold. High raw agreement can
coexist with prevalence, bias or unitising problems and is not proof of reference validity.

Stage 5 - Adjudicate with a recorded reason
After agreement has been measured, review each disagreement against the frozen source passage.
Record the adjudicated label, reason, disagreement category and any change to the codebook. A
definition change creates a new version. Do not silently overwrite a reviewer, treat majority vote
as truth or change definitions without versioning.

Stage 6 - Freeze the adjudicated reference and manifest
Before condition scoring, freeze the adjudicated labels, codebook, source bundle and manifest hash.
Create an immutable reference identifier, version and checksum plus the exact scorer input manifest.
A prior manual report or system output must never be silently adopted as truth: C0 through C3 are
evaluated conditions. Any post-freeze correction must create a new reference version and a visible
deviation record; it must not silently alter an already scored comparison.

Stage 7 - Score blind to condition where practicable
Score each condition against the same frozen reference. Hide the condition identity from scorers
where the report form and protocol make this practicable, and record unavoidable unblinding. Retain
condition-specific score records linked to the exact reference version and a blinding/deviation log.
Manual C0 and system C1, C2 and C3 outputs remain comparison outputs, not the gold reference.

Stage 8 - Enforce source-access parity and temporal eligibility
For every comparable company-period unit, use the same canonical definitions, reporting period,
eligible source window and frozen gold reference. Record inaccessible, deleted, paywalled,
login-only and later-published sources rather than pretending all evidence was available. Store
publication or availability time separately from retrieval time and the reporting cutoff. Evidence
available only after the cutoff cannot support a historical current claim. Unequal access,
inaccessible evidence or an after-cutoff passage causes a hold/abstention and a reported parity
deviation; it never becomes zero or inferred support.

Local academic basis
- artstein2008agreement: Dissertation/sources/papers/35_artstein_poesio_intercoder_agreement.pdf,
  PDF pages 2-8, 16-23 and 36-37. Supports the distinction between observed agreement,
  chance-corrected reliability, validity, coder/sample design, prevalence/bias effects and careful
  coefficient interpretation. It does not supply a universal acceptance threshold or replace
  adjudication.
- gao2023alce: Dissertation/sources/papers/28_gao_alce_citations.pdf, PDF pages 3-5 and 8-10.
  Supports separate annotation/evaluation of citation completeness and whether cited passages
  support statements. Its benchmark metrics and findings do not transfer to this project.
- pineau2021reproducibility: Dissertation/sources/papers/33_pineau_reproducibility.pdf,
  PDF pages 1-5 and 9-13. Supports complete code, data, procedure, metric and reporting records for
  reproducible empirical work; reproducibility does not itself establish validity or truth.
- gebru2021datasheets: Dissertation/sources/papers/20_gebru_datasheets_for_datasets.pdf,
  PDF pages 2 and 4-10. Supports versioned documentation of purpose, composition, sampling, splits,
  external resources, sensitive-data constraints, intended use and maintenance.
- nist2023airmf: Dissertation/sources/papers/11_nist_ai_rmf_1_0.pdf, PDF pages 32-35.
  Supports documented test sets, metrics, measurement assumptions, human-subject safeguards and
  internal-independent or external assessment. It does not certify this reference procedure.

Accessibility and limitations
The LaTeX table reads top-to-bottom as an eight-stage flow. Each stage includes an action, required
record and explicit hold/validity boundary; meaning does not rely on colour. This file is the full
linear text alternative. The table specifies a protocol and current evidence hold, not successful
execution. Two reviewers and a coefficient do not make labels true. Adjudication can preserve
domain conventions or bias, equal source access may be imperfect, later corrections may require a
new freeze, and blinding may not always be practicable. These limitations must remain visible in
the dissertation analysis.
