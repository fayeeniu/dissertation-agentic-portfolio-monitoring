    \textbf{Decision and action} &

    \midrule
    \textbf{1. Search from the problem} &
    Search from the reporting problem, research questions and clear evidence gaps. Give each possible source a planned purpose. &
    Possible title, origin and planned use. Finding a source does not make it acceptable. &
    Sources outside scope, duplicates and search snippets are not accepted. \\
    \addlinespace
    \textbf{2. Complete local copy} &
    Obtain a complete, readable local PDF or a permitted write-once capture before using it for a claim. &
    Stable local path plus origin and capture details where applicable. &
    Inaccessible, partial, snippet-only sources, or sources with no stable copy, are held or rejected. \\
    \addlinespace
    \textbf{3. Link the source records} &
    Link one citation key to its reference details, origin, local path, page count and SHA-256 checksum. &
    The source list, checksum list and bibliography agree. Web-capture records also agree when used. &
    A missing or repeated key, missing row or hash, reused path, or any mismatch blocks the source. \\
    \addlinespace
    \textbf{4. Check each claim and page} &
    Read the local full text for the exact claim and record the supporting page. &
    A claim-ledger row with the source purpose, pages and check result. &
    Claims without support, outside the source's scope or with no exact location are rejected. A citation alone does not prove truth. \\
    \addlinespace
    \textbf{5. Keep conflicts and limits} &
    Keep conflicts, unknowns, source strength, limits of reconstructed data and fields that do not apply. &
    Notes about limits and conflicts stay linked to the claim and source purpose. &
    A hidden conflict, missing warning, false equality between sources or claim that goes too far must be repaired. \\
    \addlinespace
    \textbf{6. Final source check} &
    Run the strict local check, then fix the cited files and their links for the section. &
    Readable files; matching manifest, checksums, bibliography and capture records; resolvable claim locators. &
    A missing or unreadable file, unresolved citation, changeable-only reference or any mismatch blocks the final check. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
