    \textbf{Claim precision} &
    Scored emitted claim &
    Supported emitted claims. &
    All emitted claims eligible for scoring. &
    No eligible emitted claims: \textbf{NULL / N/A} with reason, never zero. &
    Treat claims within each company and report as clustered; interpret alongside recall. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Claim recall} &
    Required gold claim &
    Correctly emitted gold claims. &
    All gold claims required by the frozen scope. &
    No required labelled gold claims: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Company/report clustering applies; omissions remain visible. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{F1} &
    Condition + frozen claim set &
    \(2PR/(P+R)\), using the precision and recall above. &
    Same frozen scoring set; no separate denominator. &
    \textbf{NULL} unless \(P\) and \(R\) are defined and \(P+R>0\). &
    Report \(P\) and \(R\) separately; F1 is not an acceptance threshold. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Unsupported-claim rate} &
    Emitted claim &
    Emitted claims without gold/evidence support. &
    All emitted claims. &
    No emitted claims: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Freeze support eligibility; do not infer support from fluency or citation presence. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Claim-support rate} &
    Emitted claim &
    Emitted claims satisfying the frozen support rule. &
    All emitted claims. &
    No emitted claims: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Keep distinct from answer correctness and provenance completeness. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Contradiction}\par\textbf{recall} &
    Gold conflict &
    Correctly flagged gold conflicts. &
    All gold conflicts. &
    No labelled gold conflicts: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Cluster linked conflicts; report false conflict flags separately. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Identity, acquisition, temporal + source quality} &
    Company--source--period layer &
    Correct identity, acquisition state, temporal eligibility and source-quality outcomes, each separate. &
    \textbf{TO FREEZE:} label-eligible units for each layer. &
    A layer with no labels is \textbf{NULL}; event outcomes are \textbf{NULL} in current D0. &
    No composite quality score; group by company/report and sourceability. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Schema + extraction} &
    Extraction record / field &
    Schema-valid output; exact or field-level match to the frozen label. &
    \textbf{TO FREEZE:} attempted versus eligible records, and record- versus field-level scope. &
    No labelled eligible records: \textbf{NULL}, never zero. &
    Stratify by format, metric type and sourceability; fields in one report are dependent. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Normalisation}\par\textbf{+ missing state} &
    Company--metric--period field &
    Correct type, value, unit or currency and correct missing-state label; retain blank--zero matrix. &
    \textbf{TO FREEZE:} labelled in-scope fields by task and data type. &
    No labels: \textbf{NULL}. Zero is a valid observation, not missing; zero accuracy requires a positive denominator. &
    Report by data type/missing class; do not pool repeated company fields as independent. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Provenance completeness} &
    Eligible emitted claim &
    Claims with source ID, locator, applicable publisher, retrieval time, checksum and period. &
    All eligible emitted claims. &
    No eligible emitted claims: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Completeness is traceability, not proof that a source or claim is true. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Locator validity} &
    Claim--locator at frozen snapshot &
    Exact locator resolves to the admitted frozen retrieval snapshot. &
    \textbf{TO FREEZE:} eligible locators/claims and treatment of multiple locators. &
    No eligible inspected locators: \textbf{NULL}; a broken inspected locator is a failure, not missing data. &
    Keep resolution separate from passage support and source truth. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Report-section completeness} &
    Report + required section &
    Required section status against the frozen reference template. &
    \textbf{TO FREEZE:} required-section set and treatment of explicit held/empty sections. &
    No frozen template/scored report: \textbf{NULL}. &
    Aggregate at report/company level; presence does not establish factual correctness. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Factual errors + severity} &
    Scored claim / report &
    Error count by pre-specified taxonomy; severity label per error. &
    Count per report; severity aggregation is \textbf{TO FREEZE} before scoring. &
    No scored report: \textbf{NULL}. Zero only after an eligible report is checked and no error is found. &
    Freeze severity definitions before condition identity/outcomes are inspected. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Transparency rate} &
    Missing, conflicted or stale item &
    Eligible items surfaced with their state and reason. &
    \textbf{TO FREEZE:} exact eligible item set and report-level aggregation. &
    No eligible labelled items: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Report missing/conflict/stale strata separately; no context-free quality score. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Human time} &
    Authorised human + report &
    Active human minutes and elapsed wall-clock minutes, recorded separately with common start/stop rules. &
    Pair the same company, period and report under one ``review-ready'' definition; aggregation is \textbf{TO FREEZE}. &
    Current D0 and unauthorised C0/C3: \textbf{NULL}; never reconstruct missing time as zero. &
    Cluster repeated tasks by reviewer and report; record tools, interruptions and waiting. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Machine stages + retries} &
    Stage, attempt and run &
    Stage duration, attempt/retry and failure/re-run events. &
    Executed stage/run; summary aggregation and failure-inclusive comparator are \textbf{TO FREEZE}. &
    Unexecuted stage: \textbf{N/A} with reason, not zero. &
    Pair same company-period; retain failures and stage boundaries. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Tokens + monetary cost} &
    Model, attempt and run &
    Input/output tokens by model/run; cost from official execution-date price, recorded formula and currency. &
    Same executed workflow unit and model/version; do not estimate an absent price. &
    Missing telemetry/price: \textbf{NULL}. Zero only when logs prove no billable usage. &
    Report tokens separately from cost; version and date bind the comparison. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Repeat reliability} &
    Fixed configuration + repeated unit &
    Deterministic outcome-vector equality; stochastic field/claim agreement; completion, schema validity and failure categories. &
    \textbf{TO FREEZE:} repeat set, vector scope, pair/denominator and stochastic agreement statistic. &
    No qualifying repeats or denominator zero: \textbf{NULL}, never zero. &
    Cluster by company/report; report failures and configuration hash; no universal threshold. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{C3 usability + edit burden} &
    Authorised reviewer + task/report &
    Ethics-approved task success, time, reversals, confidence calibration or licensed scale; edit events/sections and optional character/semantic distance. &
    \textbf{TO FREEZE} in the authorised instrument/task protocol; retain C2 pre-review and C3 post-review versions. &
    Current D0/no participant observation: \textbf{NULL}; do not synthesize reviewer utility. &
    Cluster by reviewer/report. Log order and expertise; choose no scale after outcomes. \\
