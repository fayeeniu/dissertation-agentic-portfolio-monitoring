    \textbf{Claim precision} &
    Scored emitted claim &
    Supported emitted claims. &
    All emitted claims eligible for scoring. &
    No eligible emitted claims: \textbf{NULL / N/A} with reason, never zero. &
    Treat claims from the same company or report as related; interpret with recall. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Claim recall} &
    Required reference claim &
    Required reference claims produced correctly. &
    All reference claims required by the fixed scope. &
    No required labelled reference claims: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Group by company and report; missing claims remain visible. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{F1} &
    Condition + fixed claim set &
    \(2PR/(P+R)\), using the precision and recall above. &
    Same fixed scoring set; no separate total. &
    \textbf{NULL} unless \(P\) and \(R\) are defined and \(P+R>0\). &
    Report \(P\) and \(R\) separately; F1 is not an acceptance threshold. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Unsupported-claim rate} &
    Emitted claim &
    Produced claims without reference or evidence support. &
    All emitted claims. &
    No emitted claims: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Fix the support rules before scoring. Good writing or a citation does not prove support. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Claim-support rate} &
    Emitted claim &
    Produced claims that meet the fixed support rule. &
    All emitted claims. &
    No emitted claims: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Keep separate from answer correctness and complete source records. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Contradiction}\par\textbf{recall} &
    Reference conflict &
    Reference conflicts flagged correctly. &
    All reference conflicts. &
    No labelled reference conflicts: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Group linked conflicts; report false conflict flags separately. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Identity, acquisition, temporal + source quality} &
    Company--source--period layer &
    Correct identity, acquisition state, temporal eligibility and source-quality outcomes, each separate. &
    \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY:} units with labels for each layer. &
    A layer with no labels is \textbf{NULL}; event outcomes are \textbf{NULL} in current D0. &
    No single quality score; group by company, report and whether a source can be found. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Schema + extraction} &
    Extraction record / field &
    Output that follows the schema; exact or field-level match to the fixed label. &
    \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY:} attempted and eligible records, and record-level or field-level scope. &
    No labelled eligible records: \textbf{NULL}, never zero. &
    Report by format, measure type and whether a source can be found. Fields in one report are related. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Normalisation}\par\textbf{+ missing state} &
    Company--metric--period field &
    Correct type, value, unit or currency and correct missing-state label; retain blank--zero matrix. &
    \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY:} labelled fields in scope by task and data type. &
    No labels: \textbf{NULL}. Zero is a valid observation, not missing; zero accuracy requires a positive denominator. &
    Report by data type/missing class; do not pool repeated company fields as independent. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Complete source records} &
    Eligible emitted claim &
    Claims with source ID, locator, applicable publisher, retrieval time, checksum and period. &
    All eligible emitted claims. &
    No eligible emitted claims: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Completeness makes a claim traceable. It does not prove that the source or claim is true. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Source-location validity} &
    Claim and location in a fixed source copy &
    Exact location opens the accepted fixed source copy. &
    \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY:} eligible locations and claims, and how several locations are handled. &
    No eligible inspected locators: \textbf{NULL}; a broken inspected locator is a failure, not missing data. &
    Keep resolution separate from passage support and source truth. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Report-section completeness} &
    Report + required section &
    Required section status against the fixed reference template. &
    \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY:} required sections and how held or empty sections are handled. &
    No fixed template or scored report: \textbf{NULL}. &
    Aggregate at report/company level; presence does not establish factual correctness. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Factual errors + severity} &
    Scored claim / report &
    Error count using categories set in advance; seriousness label for each error. &
    Count per report; the rule for combining seriousness is \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY}. &
    No scored report: \textbf{NULL}. Zero only after an eligible report is checked and no error is found. &
    Fix seriousness definitions before the scorer sees the condition or outcomes. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Transparency rate} &
    Missing, conflicted or stale item &
    Eligible items surfaced with their state and reason. &
    \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY:} exact eligible items and how report-level results are combined. &
    No eligible labelled items: \textbf{NULL / N/A}, never zero. &
    Report missing, conflict and old-information groups separately. Do not use one score without context. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Human time} &
    Authorised human + report &
    Active human minutes and elapsed wall-clock minutes, recorded separately with common start/stop rules. &
    Match the same company, period and report under one ``review-ready'' definition. How results are combined is \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY}. &
    Current D0 and unauthorised C0/C3: \textbf{NULL}; never reconstruct missing time as zero. &
    Group repeated tasks by reviewer and report. Record tools, interruptions and waiting. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Machine stages + retries} &
    Stage, attempt and run &
    Stage duration, attempt/retry and failure/re-run events. &
    Executed stage or run; summary method and a comparison that includes failures are \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY}. &
    Unexecuted stage: \textbf{N/A} with reason, not zero. &
    Pair same company-period; retain failures and stage boundaries. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Tokens + monetary cost} &
    Model, attempt and run &
    Input/output tokens by model/run; cost from official execution-date price, recorded formula and currency. &
    Same executed workflow unit and model/version; do not estimate an absent price. &
    Missing logs or price: \textbf{NULL}. Zero only when logs prove there was no billable use. &
    Report tokens separately from cost; version and date bind the comparison. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{Repeat reliability} &
    Fixed configuration + repeated unit &
    Complete equality for fixed outputs; field or claim agreement for settings that can vary; completion, schema validity and failure categories. &
    \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY:} repeat set, output scope, matched total and agreement measure for settings that can vary. &
    No qualifying repeats or denominator zero: \textbf{NULL}, never zero. &
    Group by company and report. Show failures and configuration hash. There is no universal pass level. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{C3 usability + edit burden} &
    Authorised reviewer + task/report &
    Ethics-approved task success, time, changed decisions, whether confidence matches correctness, or a licensed scale; edit events and optional text or meaning-based distance. &
    \textbf{TO DECIDE BEFORE STUDY} in the authorised questionnaire and task plan. Keep C2 before review and C3 after review. &
    Current D0 with no participant observation: \textbf{NULL}. Do not invent reviewer value. &
    Group by reviewer and report. Record order and experience. Do not choose a scale after seeing outcomes. \\
