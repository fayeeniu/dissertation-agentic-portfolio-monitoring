Title
Requirements and trust-boundary matrix for the research prototype

Author and status
Faye Niu. Author synthesis. Conceptual and non-empirical; this is not a result.

Purpose
This matrix separates the actor, data and action permitted inside each boundary from the
implemented control, approved outstanding work, explicit non-goal or stop condition, and the
local evidence that supports the status. "Implemented" means that named repository code exists
and selected local checks exercise the stated mechanism. It does not mean that live company
research, restricted-data processing, a participant study, security effectiveness or a production
deployment has been completed or authorised.

Linear reading order

1. Local runtime and accountable actor

Boundary or requirement
NFR-SEC-001 and FR-UI-003--004 define a local, single-user runtime and accountable review actor.

In-scope actor, data and action
One locally configured researcher or reviewer uses the FastAPI/Jinja interface. SQLite and
ignored local snapshot/export directories hold the permitted local state.

Current implemented control
The CLI refuses a non-loopback host outside the documented Docker-local mode. HTTP middleware
checks client and Host values, applies security headers, and requires a double-submit CSRF value
for mutations. Reviewer identity comes from local configuration rather than a form field. Docker
mode accepts private container-network clients only when the published host port remains bound to
loopback.

Approved outstanding work
A formal accessibility/security review and any wider deployment design require separate evidence
and authority.

Explicit non-goal or stop condition
Anonymous, remote and multi-user access are outside this prototype. Reject a non-loopback client,
unexpected Host, missing CSRF value or missing configured reviewer. Loopback and CSRF are not a
substitute for production authentication, authorisation or tenant isolation.

Evidence locator and status
src/portfolio_agent/cli.py, function main, serve branch; src/portfolio_agent/web.py, create_app
security middleware, require_csrf and reviewer_identity; tests/integration/test_web.py,
test_web_rejects_csrf_host_forgery_and_form_actor and
test_container_local_mode_accepts_only_private_container_clients. Status: IMPLEMENTED LOCAL-ONLY;
not production authentication.

2. Legal identity first

Boundary or requirement
FR-ID-001--004 and FR-INT-001/006 require exact, source-scoped identity and a named decision.

In-scope actor, data and action
A submitted Companies House number is an identity claim. A configured reviewer can accept or
reject the identifier with a substantive rationale.

Current implemented control
A structurally valid number can be submitted without a name or website, but it creates an
unresolved company and an unreviewed Companies House identifier. Company names and domains do not
auto-merge legal identity. A classification mismatch, conflicting identifier or unresolved state
fails closed. Decisions and their actor/rationale remain auditable.

Approved outstanding work
Before direct live registry use, Gate G2 still requires current authoritative interface and terms
evidence, credential and storage approval, exact identifier mapping, time/cutoff semantics,
retention and attribution review.

Explicit non-goal or stop condition
Structural validity is not legal-identity authority. Do not collect, link or report company facts
while identity is unresolved, conflicting, rejected or belongs to another classification boundary.
Do not use a name, website or filename as an automatic legal-identity merge.

Evidence locator and status
src/portfolio_agent/company_intelligence.py, CompanyIntakeService.create,
CompanyIntakeService.decide_identifier and _select_or_create_company; docs/adr/0007-offline-
company-intelligence-foundation.md, Decision items 4--6 and 10;
tests/integration/test_company_intelligence.py,
test_number_only_intake_is_idempotent_and_requires_named_review. Status: IMPLEMENTED OFFLINE;
live Companies House access is held.

3. Classification and data authority

Boundary or requirement
NFR-SEC-002--005 and NFR-RES-001 separate restricted/internal, public and synthetic data and their
permitted flows.

In-scope actor, data and action
Restricted or internal portfolio material remains purpose-limited inside local ignored storage.
Admitted public evidence and fictional synthetic cases use their separate permitted routes.

Current implemented control
DataClassification is a closed enum containing public, internal, restricted and synthetic.
Purpose, actor and classification are retained with intake records. Create-once local files use
mode 0600 and are ignored by Git. Traces retain bounded identifiers, counts and hashes rather than
raw restricted content. The external-provider boundary rechecks classification and untrusted
content.

Approved outstanding work
Before real restricted-data or participant work, verify lawful authority, controller, purpose,
access list, retention/deletion plan and any required ethics, information or consent material.

Explicit non-goal or stop condition
Never place restricted or internal content in Git, synthetic fixtures, raw traces or an external
model. Unclear authority, an unexpected personal field or an unapproved processor stops
processing. The repository does not establish ethics approval or restricted-data authority.

Evidence locator and status
src/portfolio_agent/enums.py, DataClassification; src/portfolio_agent/security.py,
assert_external_model_safe; docs/SECURITY_AND_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md, Data classification and
permitted flow, Local storage controls and Human/participant and ethics hold;
tests/unit/test_security.py and tests/integration/test_llm_boundary.py. Status: CONTROL
IMPLEMENTED; authority and ethics evidence NOT VERIFIED.

4. Public discovery and optional external model

Boundary or requirement
FR-EXT-004--008 and FR-RES-001--004 bound discovery, public capture and optional extraction.

In-scope actor, data and action
Only a public research case with a named-reviewed Companies House number and cutoff may enter the
bounded company-research workflow. Web search proposes HTTPS URL candidates. Captured public text
or synthetic evidence may enter optional extraction; synthetic fixtures rehearse the path without
an outbound call.

Current implemented control
External-model and live-retrieval flags default closed and must open together with a configured
reviewer and key. Search snippets and free-form model prose are not evidence. Public capture is
bounded by HTTPS/network, redirects, robots, MIME, timeout and byte controls. Instruction-like
content is rejected. External extraction uses store=False, an enforced model route, bounded
attempts and strict schema/exact-span grounding; deterministic normalisation and independent
verification remain mandatory.

Approved outstanding work
The live public-company path still requires separate authorisation and empirical evaluation.
Source-specific G2 review and execution-time provider-control verification remain outstanding.
Direct live Companies House or other registry connectors remain a P1 item.

Explicit non-goal or stop condition
No restricted/internal model input, public-person profiling, arbitrary browsing, snippet-supported
claim, unrestricted crawling, investment recommendation, or model approval/export. Missing, held,
post-cutoff, untrusted or conflicting evidence cannot become a supported claim.

Evidence locator and status
src/portfolio_agent/config.py, Settings defaults; src/portfolio_agent/bootstrap.py,
create_runtime gate checks; src/portfolio_agent/security.py; docs/adr/0008-bounded-live-company-
research.md, Decision and Consequences; tests/integration/test_llm_boundary.py,
test_restricted_content_is_rejected_before_client_invocation and
test_default_runtime_refuses_open_external_gates_before_database_creation;
tests/integration/test_company_research.py,
test_live_research_rejects_non_public_case_and_mismatched_runtime_flags. Status: CODE AND LOCAL/FAKE
BOUNDARY TEST EVIDENCE. No live public-company research execution or result is claimed, and this
matrix does not represent web-search integration as complete.

5. Named review and export

Boundary or requirement
FR-VER-002 and FR-REP-004--009 require independent verification, named review and a version-bound
export gate.

In-scope actor, data and action
The configured reviewer assesses a versioned report or profile whose candidate claims have
verification records, and then approves, rejects or requests changes.

Current implemented control
Approval records actor, reason, version and time. An optimistic lock rejects a stale action.
Editing creates a new version, revokes approval and requires review again. Export is forbidden
before approval, uses a pending manifest plus staging/atomic finalisation, and rechecks content and
file hashes before reuse.

Approved outstanding work
Authorised C0/C3 observation and reviewer-utility evidence remain pending. Production identities,
roles or signed approval would need a separately scoped architecture and authority.

Explicit non-goal or stop condition
No model or workflow stage can approve or publish. Missing verification, stale versions, rejected
reports, absent approval, tampered bundles or incomplete writes stop export. Test approval on a
synthetic report is mechanism evidence, not participant or user-benefit evidence.

Evidence locator and status
src/portfolio_agent/reporting.py, ReportService.approve, edit_section, export,
_assert_approval_ready and _verified_export_bundle; tests/integration/test_reporting.py,
test_export_is_blocked_until_audited_human_approval,
test_section_edit_creates_version_and_requires_reapproval and
test_finalized_export_is_reverified_before_reuse. Status: IMPLEMENTED ON SYNTHETIC/OFFLINE FLOWS;
authorised human-study result unavailable.

6. Prototype scope and explicit non-goals

Boundary or requirement
The Project Charter P0/P1/P2 boundaries define what this engineering artefact may demonstrate.

In-scope actor, data and action
The prototype supports bounded ingestion, typed evidence, independent verification and local named
review as engineering mechanisms for RQ1--RQ3.

Current implemented control
Fixed stage contracts, explicit missing/failed/held states, provenance and local audit records make
unsupported actions visible rather than silently expanding scope.

Approved outstanding work
Live connectors, participant work, restricted-data evaluation or deployment may proceed only
through their named future gates and separate authority. They are not completion criteria for the
current slice.

Explicit non-goal or stop condition
Do not use the supplied dashboard credential. The current scope excludes production
authentication/tenancy, social scraping, unrestricted crawling, autonomous publication,
investment advice or scoring, fine-tuning, cloud deployment and native PPTX/PDF generation.

Evidence locator and status
docs/PROJECT_CHARTER.md, P0 scope, Explicit non-goals, Constraints and Change control;
docs/REQUIREMENTS.md, P1 backlog and P2 exclusions; docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, Scalability and production
gaps. Status: CONTRACT BOUNDARY, not an empirical result.

Limitations
- This table contains no empirical value, participant observation, coverage result, cost, timing,
  source-quality score or production claim.
- Local tests establish only the exercised contracts. They do not establish legal compliance,
  ethics approval, data authority, live source availability, model quality, security effectiveness
  or external validity.
- Offline Companies House syntax/intake does not prove current legal identity or registry truth.
- The existence of bounded research code and fake/local tests does not establish completed or
  authorised live web-search integration.
- store=False is not described as zero data retention and does not replace data-processing
  authority or minimisation.
- The named reviewer in local configuration is an accountable prototype actor, not a production
  authenticated principal or evidence of an authorised participant study.
