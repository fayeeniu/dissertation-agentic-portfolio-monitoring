SYS-F3 - Legal-identity intake and decision flow
Author: Faye Niu
Target section: 4.4 Intake and legal-identity control
Suggested LaTeX label: fig:sys-legal-identity-decision-flow
Status: author synthesis; conceptual, non-empirical, and not a result.

Purpose
This figure explains how the prototype receives portfolio records and company-research cases, preserves the submitted bytes or identity claims, and prevents downstream research until a named reviewer accepts an exact Companies House number as the legal-identity anchor. It separates locally tested implementation controls from an implemented but live-unrun public-web research route and a held direct Companies House retrieval route. It does not claim authoritative live identity resolution, perfect deduplication, source completeness, external identity-API use, or production concurrency.

Status key
1. Solid blue boundary - IMPLEMENTED + LOCAL TESTS. The schema or service control exists and is exercised by local unit, integration, migration, or API tests. This is engineering evidence, not a production or empirical-performance claim.
2. Dashed amber boundary - IMPLEMENTED / LIVE UNRUN. The bounded public-web research mechanics have fake, synthetic, or adversarial test coverage, but no completed live public-company run or external-model result is claimed.
3. Dotted red boundary - HELD / GATE CLOSED. Direct live Companies House registry retrieval remains held by the source-admission G2 decision. Synthetic registry replay does not grant live authority.

Reading order

1. Two intake routes, one create-once and idempotent boundary - implemented and locally tested.

   Portfolio file import:
   - The PortfolioImporter accepts XLSX, CSV, or canonical JSON together with a reporting period, reporting cutoff where supplied, and classification.
   - It hashes the supplied bytes, derives a period-bound dataset identity, writes a restrictive local raw snapshot, and records a RawSubmission with filename, checksum, path, classification, period and catalogue/profile metadata.
   - The same admitted bytes for the same period reuse the existing import rather than creating a second dataset or rewriting the raw file.
   - A company name and supplied external identifier remain submitted identity claims. They are not assumed to identify the same legal entity only because a normalised name matches.

   Company-research case intake:
   - A structurally valid Companies House number is sufficient to create a first-class company case. A name or website is not required.
   - Website, name plus jurisdiction, or a declared-company document may also be submitted, but they remain reviewable claims. They do not resolve legal identity automatically.
   - The service creates a stable IntakeArtifact fingerprint from the intake-rule version, template version, input kind, purpose, data class and agreed submitted values. An identical fingerprint reuses the existing intake record, company and case.
   - Document bytes receive a SHA-256, safe basename, classification, purpose and actor. The create-once local file is mode 0600. Existing bytes are verified before reuse, conflicting concurrent bytes fail, and a failed database transaction removes only the newly created snapshot.
   - Uploaded content is recorded as untrusted, is not executed, and is not sent to an external model by this intake step.

2. Identity claims feed an exact-number, named decision gate - implemented and locally tested.

   Exact Companies House number claim:
   - The implementation applies Unicode normalisation, trims the input, changes letters to upper case, removes spaces and hyphens, and adds leading zeroes to a number of up to eight digits.
   - The conservative structural rule accepts exactly eight digits or a two-letter prefix followed by six digits. Structural validity is not authoritative proof that a live registry record exists or that the company is currently operating.
   - The normalised value is unique within its closed identifier scheme and belongs to Companies House. A new public identifier remains reviewed=false until a named decision.

   Supporting claims without legal-merge authority:
   - Company names are normalised only to order or suggest possible matches. The same normalised name may appear more than once. There is no fuzzy matcher and no automatic merge based only on a name.
   - An HTTPS website is canonicalized into a domain claim with its own pending, verified, or rejected decision history. Verifying a domain does not mark the company legal identity as resolved.
   - A document is checksum-addressed local evidence tied to a declared company claim. It remains untrusted and cannot establish legal identity by itself.

   Named authority review:
   - The decision checks exact scheme-plus-number uniqueness, classification boundaries, candidate state, existing identifier ownership and any submitted-name mismatch.
   - Accept or reject requires a configured named actor and substantive rationale. The decision is appended to IdentityDecision or CompanyIdentifierDecision history rather than silently overwriting the submitted claim.
   - A stale or already final identifier/domain decision is refused. An accepted identity cannot cross a data-classification boundary or attach a number already owned by another company.
   - This is a local human decision over persisted claims. It is not a hidden live Companies House lookup.

   Outcomes:
   - RESOLVED / ACCEPTED: the exact number is reviewed, Company.resolution_status becomes resolved, and the associated ResearchCase becomes ready. Only this outcome may proceed to the downstream eligibility gate.
   - CONFLICT / AMBIGUITY HOLD: identifier/name disagreement, same-name/different-number collision, classification conflict, or duplicate identifier ownership prevents an automatic merge. The candidate remains held for named review. Imported observations remain attached and auditable rather than being discarded.
   - UNRESOLVED / REJECTED: missing, structurally invalid, pending, or rejected authority leaves the company unresolved/candidate and the ResearchCase in identity_hold.

3. Downstream research requires reviewed legal identity - fail closed.
   - CompanyResearchService checks four persisted conditions together: the case classification is public; the case status is ready; the company resolution status is resolved; and the company has a reviewed Companies House-number identifier.
   - If any condition is absent, the service returns an identity/classification hold before creating or advancing research. A name, verified domain, document, or structurally valid but unreviewed number cannot bypass this gate.
   - When all conditions hold and the separately configured external gates are open, the bounded public-web route can perform URL discovery, guarded capture and exact-span extraction. The implementation has fake, synthetic, and adversarial engineering tests, but no completed live company or model run is claimed.
   - Direct Companies House registry retrieval is a different source path. Its connector and temporal/provenance mechanisms have synthetic replay tests, while live access remains G2 HELD pending current access, licence/terms, credential, retention, attribution and exact-identifier review. Synthetic replay is not live source admission.

Concurrency and integrity boundary
- Database uniqueness constraints protect the exact identifier scheme/value and intake fingerprint within the local database.
- Create-once file creation verifies competing bytes, and final decision state refuses a stale second decision. Other review/report paths also use local optimistic version checks where specified.
- These controls reduce duplicate or stale local writes. They do not make SQLite and the local filesystem a multi-user production identity service, distributed transaction system, or authoritative deduplication platform.

Explicit limitations and non-goals
- No authoritative live legal-identity resolution is asserted.
- No fuzzy, name-only, domain-only or document-only automatic company merge is implemented.
- No perfect deduplication, external identity API, hosted identity service, multi-user tenancy, distributed locking or production concurrency guarantee is claimed.
- A Companies House number anchors source-scoped legal identity. Registry presence does not by itself establish current trading activity, economic performance, ownership, complete filings, source truth or source fitness for every use.
