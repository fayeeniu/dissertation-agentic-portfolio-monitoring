    \textbf{1. Define}\par Codebook + source policy &
    Create a versioned labelling guide from the data dictionary, with examples for measures, claims, sources and periods. Define which sources may be used and the cut-off date. &
    Labelling-guide version and hash; label schema; source rules and source-list template. &
    Hold labelling and scoring until the scope, examples, source rules and cut-off are clear. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{2. Label}\par Work separately &
    Two authorised reviewers with relevant subject knowledge label the same fixed set of sources independently. Keep their records separate until agreement is measured. &
    Reviewer IDs; source-set version; separate structured labels and times. &
    Unauthorised reviewer, different source set or discussion before the separate labels are complete: \textbf{HOLD}. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{3. Record}\par Complete label &
    Record the agreed value or reason it is missing, whether evidence is eligible, whether a source can be found, the period, claim support and required report section. &
    Structured label with source and location, plus a clear missing, unclear or conflict state. &
    Good writing or a citation does not prove support. Unsupported or unclear items remain held. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{4. Measure}\par Before discussion &
    Report raw agreement. Add a statistic corrected for chance only where the sample, label scale and task design support its use. &
    Total number of eligible labels; direct agreement; suitable adjusted measure, assumptions and uncertainty; otherwise \textbf{NULL}. &
    Do not import a pass threshold. High direct agreement can hide an uneven label balance, bias or differences in how text was divided. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{5. Agree final labels}\par Give reasons &
    Compare each disagreement with the fixed passage. Record the reason for the final choice and any change to the labelling guide. &
    Final label; reason; type of disagreement; revised guide where needed. &
    No silent change, no assumption that a majority is true, and no change to a definition without a new version. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{6. Fix}\par Reference + source list &
    Fix the final reference answers, labelling guide, source set and source-list hash before scoring any condition. &
    Write-once reference ID, version and hash, plus the scorer's input list. &
    Manual or system output is never assumed to be true. A later change needs a new version and a record of the change. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{7. Score}\par Blind where practicable &
    Score conditions against the same fixed reference without showing the scorer the condition where practical. Record any case where the condition becomes known. &
    Scores for each condition linked to the reference; record of blind scoring and any departure from it. &
    C0 manual and C1-C3 system outputs are conditions being tested, not the reference answer. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{8. Keep equal}\par Sources + time &
    Give conditions the same definitions, period, eligible source window and reference answers. Record inaccessible, deleted, paywalled, sign-in-only and later sources. Keep publication and availability separate from collection time. &
    Company-period source list; source state; publication, availability, collection and cut-off times; any difference in access. &
    Unequal access or support after the cut-off: hold the claim and report the difference. Never change missing evidence to zero. \\
