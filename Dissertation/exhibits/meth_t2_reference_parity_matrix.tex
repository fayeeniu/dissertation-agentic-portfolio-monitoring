    \toprule
    \textbf{Stage / gate} &
    \textbf{Controlled action} &
    \textbf{Required record or output} &
    \textbf{Hold / validity boundary} \\
    \midrule

    \textbf{1. Define}\par Codebook + source policy &
    Version a codebook from the data dictionary with metric, claim, source and period examples. Define eligible-source rules and an availability cutoff. &
    Codebook version/hash; label schema; source-policy and eligible-source-manifest template. &
    Hold labelling and scoring until scope, examples, source eligibility and cutoff are explicit. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{2. Label}\par Independent pass &
    Two authorised domain reviewers independently label the same frozen source bundle. Preserve separate records until agreement is measured. &
    Reviewer IDs; bundle/version; independent typed labels and timestamps. &
    Unauthorised reviewer, unequal bundle or consensus during the independent pass: \textbf{HOLD}. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{3. Capture}\par Complete label &
    Record canonical value or missing state, evidence eligibility, sourceability, period, claim support and report-section requirement. &
    Typed label with source/locator plus explicit missing, ambiguous or conflict state. &
    Do not infer support from fluency or citation presence; unsupported or ambiguous items remain held. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{4. Measure}\par Before adjudication &
    Report raw agreement. Add a statistic corrected for chance only where the sample, label scale and task design support its use. &
    Denominator; raw agreement; task-appropriate coefficient, assumptions and uncertainty; otherwise \textbf{NULL}. &
    No imported acceptance threshold. High raw agreement can conceal prevalence, bias or unitising effects. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{5. Adjudicate}\par Reasoned resolution &
    Compare each disagreement with the frozen passage. Record the resolution reason and any codebook revision/version. &
    Adjudicated label; reason; disagreement category; revised codebook where needed. &
    No silent overwrite, majority-as-truth rule or unversioned definition change. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{6. Freeze}\par Reference + manifest &
    Freeze the adjudicated reference, codebook, source bundle and manifest hash before condition scoring. &
    Immutable reference ID/version/hash and scorer input manifest. &
    Manual or system output is never adopted as truth. Post-freeze change requires a new version and deviation record. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{7. Score}\par Blind where practicable &
    Score conditions against the same frozen reference, blind to condition where practicable; record any unavoidable unblinding. &
    Condition-specific score records linked to the reference; blinding/deviation log. &
    C0 manual and C1--C3 system outputs are evaluated conditions, not the gold reference. \\

    \addlinespace[1.5pt]
    \textbf{8. Enforce}\par Parity + time &
    Give comparable conditions the same definitions, period, eligible source window and gold reference. Record inaccessible, deleted, paywalled, login-only and later-published states. Separate publication/availability from retrieval time. &
    Company-period access manifest; source state; publication/availability, retrieval and cutoff times; parity deviations. &
    Unequal access or after-cutoff support: hold/abstain and report the deviation; never convert missing evidence to zero. \\
