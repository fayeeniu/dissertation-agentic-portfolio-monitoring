METH-F3 - Analysis decision flow
Target section: 3.6 Statistical and qualitative analysis
Suggested LaTeX label: fig:meth-analysis-decision-flow

Purpose and evidence boundary
This is Faye Niu's conceptual, non-empirical synthesis of the current draft analysis plan. It shows
the decisions required before quantitative comparisons or qualitative interpretation can be
reported. It does not choose the final statistical method or sample size and contains no result,
effect estimate, confidence interval, p-value, sample count, acceptance threshold, time saving,
cost, participant benefit or production-performance claim.

Reading order

Shared accounting gate - flow accounting before analysis
Record eligible, excluded, failed and scored units for every condition. Each category retains its
denominator and reason. Publish denominators, descriptive distributions and missingness before an
inferential choice. A failed unit is not silently removed from the evidence base, and an exclusion
must be justified rather than selected because of its outcome.

The accounting gate then separates into two parallel branches. The branches answer different
questions and meet only at integrated interpretation and explicit limitations.

Quantitative branch

Q1 - denominators and descriptives
State the per-condition denominators, distributions and missingness. No statistical test precedes
flow accounting. A null or inapplicable outcome remains identified rather than being recoded as
zero.

Q2 - dependence and unit check
Pair conditions on the same company-period or claim when they share units. Repeated fields and
claims within one report or company are dependent observations, not independent replicates.
Analysis therefore works at the report/company unit or uses a cluster-aware interval whose
resampling and clustering unit is declared and supported by the design.

Q3 - conditional method family
For paired binary outcomes, an exact paired method may be considered. For non-normal paired time
or edit outcomes, a paired Wilcoxon-style analysis may be considered only when the sample and
assumptions support it. These are candidate method families, not selected tests. The diagram does
not imply that either family will be used or that any assumption has already passed.

Q4 - effect and uncertainty
Report the effect size and an uncertainty interval rather than a p-value alone. Name the unit of
analysis, estimator, assumptions, failures and interval construction. A bootstrap interval is not
automatically valid: its resampling unit, dependence model, interval procedure and attainable
coverage must fit the executed design.

Q5 - multiplicity, strata and sensitivity
Predeclare multiplicity handling when several hypotheses are confirmatory; otherwise label the
analysis exploratory. Report strata only when their sample and design support interpretation.
Repeat the main summaries with failed units and justified exclusions included as applicable.
Preserve negative and null findings and unexpected regressions instead of reporting only favourable
effects.

Qualitative branch

K1 - authorised inputs only
Admit the predefined error taxonomy and, only where separately authorised and consented, reviewer
feedback. Do not infer participant evidence from synthetic fixtures and do not use reviewer
feedback without the required authority and consent.

K2 - predefined code families
- ID: wrong company, duplicate merge or unresolved alias.
- EXT: omitted field, fabricated field or wrong source span.
- NORM: wrong type, unit or currency; blank-versus-zero error; ratio scaling error.
- TIME: future evidence, stale value, wrong period or filing-lag error.
- PROV: missing locator, checksum mismatch or incomplete citation.
- VER: verifier miss, false rejection or unsupported acceptance.
- REP: missing required section or inconsistent narrative.
- SEC: unsafe instruction-following, secret exposure or malformed input handling.
- HITL: harmful edit, missed exception or ungrounded approval.

K3 - case-by-code framework matrix
Use cases as rows and the predefined code families, plus justified additions, as columns. Each
entry retains the exact report, claim, event or feedback locator. The matrix supports comparison
within and across cases; it does not convert qualitative records into population prevalence.

K4 - versioned coding and disagreement
Record the coder, codebook and version, applied code, rationale, disagreement and resolution.
Do not silently overwrite a first judgement. Agreement is evidence about coding consistency under
the stated task and assumptions; it is not proof of source truth or claim validity.

K5 - patterns, negative cases and limitations
Compare patterns within and across cases. Retain contradictions, minority patterns, exceptions and
negative cases. Report consent, data coverage, coder or researcher influence and unresolved
disagreement. Counts from purposive feedback must not be presented as population frequencies or
generalised prevalence.

Integration
Combine quantitative effect and uncertainty statements with qualitative patterns and negative
cases only at an integrated interpretation stage. Keep the executed evidence, evidence class,
failed or excluded units, unresolved holds and protocol deviations visible in the limitations.
Neither branch is allowed to erase contrary evidence from the other.

Explicit stop - TO FREEZE
The exact statistical method and sample size are not selected now. They remain TO FREEZE after D1
variance and annotation evidence has been obtained and after supervisor and statistical review.
No inferential result is asserted by this exhibit or by the current protocol-only choice set.

Local literature basis
- demsar2006comparisons: Dissertation/sources/papers/36_demsar_statistical_comparisons.pdf,
  PDF pages 5-9. Supports checking the experimental unit, dependence and assumptions before using
  paired or non-parametric comparisons. Its multi-dataset classifier setting does not select a test
  for this company/report study, and repeated runs must not be treated as independent cases.
- diciccio1996bootstrap: Dissertation/sources/papers/38_diciccio_efron_bootstrap_confidence_intervals.pdf,
  PDF pages 1-4. Supports interval accuracy and coverage being method- and assumption-dependent.
  It does not establish a cluster-aware interval or resampling unit for this study.
- gale2013framework: Dissertation/sources/papers/37_gale_framework_method_qualitative_analysis.pdf,
  PDF pages 3-7. Supports coding, an analytical framework, case-by-code charting, cross-case
  comparison, interpretation and an audit trail. Its health-research examples require careful
  transfer and do not justify population-frequency claims about software errors or feedback.
- artstein2008agreement: Dissertation/sources/papers/35_artstein_poesio_intercoder_agreement.pdf,
  PDF pages 2-8 and 26-28. Supports distinguishing coding agreement from validity and recording
  coder and unit decisions. Agreement does not prove truth and requires task-appropriate assumptions.
- kapoor2023leakage: Dissertation/sources/papers/32_kapoor_data_leakage.pdf,
  PDF pages 1-7. Supports separating analysis choices from held-out evidence and accounting for
  dependence and leakage risks. It does not show that this study has executed its final evaluation.
- pineau2021reproducibility: Dissertation/sources/papers/33_pineau_reproducibility.pdf,
  PDF pages 1-5. Supports complete reporting of metrics, procedures, code and outcomes, including
  information needed to assess reproducibility. Reproducibility does not establish external validity.
- nist2023airmf: Dissertation/sources/papers/11_nist_ai_rmf_1_0.pdf,
  PDF pages 33-35. Supports rigorous testing, uncertainty reporting, documented limitations and
  independent review. The voluntary framework does not certify this artefact or its analysis.

Accessibility and limitations
The current visual is a monochrome PNG screenshot generated from the corresponding Mermaid source.
The meaning of every branch, method family, code family, stop and limitation is written in text, so
the figure does not depend on colour; this file is the complete linear reading-order alternative.

This exhibit is an analysis decision contract, not an empirical result. It does not establish that
D1 has run, that the final gold reference exists, that the D2 holdout has opened, that C0-C3 have
been executed, that participant feedback has been collected, or that a statistical assumption has
passed. Exact sample size, estimator, hypothesis family, interval method, clustering or resampling
unit, multiplicity procedure, strata and qualitative-coding adjudication remain subject to the
future protocol freeze and evidence available at execution. Preserving negative, null and regressive
outcomes reduces selective reporting risk but does not itself guarantee validity or generalisation.
