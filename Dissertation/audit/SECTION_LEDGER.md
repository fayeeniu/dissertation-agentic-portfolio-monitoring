1. verify claim-fit pages in locally stored sources;
2. `$dissertation-expert` drafts the subsection and any evidence-backed exhibit;
3. run citation, source, LaTeX, and narrow evidence checks;
4. `$dissertation-reviewer` reviews the actual subsection;
5. `$dissertation-expert` resolves all blocker/major findings;
6. re-review until `gate: PASS` before the next subsection begins.
