\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Early-stage portfolio reporting combines heterogeneous submissions with incomplete and changing public evidence. This creates risks of entity mismatch, blank-versus-zero errors, temporally ineligible facts, hidden contradictions, and fluent but unsupported narrative. This dissertation investigates the extent to which a bounded multi-agent artificial-intelligence workflow can automate data ingestion, validation, and report generation while preserving traceability, reliability, and human authority. It defines deterministic, independent-verification, manual, and human-review comparisons rather than assuming that additional agents improve quality.

The implemented research artefact is a loopback-only Python/FastAPI/SQLite application. Typed contracts preserve source classification, reporting periods, missing states, immutable hashes, and exact claim-to-evidence provenance. A fixed workflow separates planning, identity resolution, collection, extraction, normalisation, independent verification, composition, and human review. An implemented but live-unrun Company 360 case study accepts a Companies House number as its required intake, holds legal identity for named review, and uses OpenAI web search only to discover candidate URLs. Local controls include connection-to-validated-public-IP pinning with hostname TLS verification, guarded capture, immutable snapshots, bounded public-text extraction, exact-span validation, deterministic deck composition, and named approval. Classification gates are tested to block restricted or internal material from external-model processing. The scope excludes investment recommendations and speculative valuation, and documented pattern controls reject tested forms; this does not demonstrate universal enforcement across all formulations.

Engineering evaluation used fourteen labelled fictional cases covering missingness, mixed types and currencies, identity ambiguity, stale or inaccessible evidence, contradictions, duplicate support, unsupported claims, and prompt injection. Across three deterministic repetitions, the independent-verifier condition matched all expected case outcomes, with precision, recall, and F1 of 1.00, provenance completeness of 1.00, and an unsupported-claim rate of 0.00. The no-verifier baseline produced precision of 0.455, F1 of 0.625, provenance completeness of 0.818, and an unsupported-claim rate of 0.545. These are reproducible synthetic mechanism measurements, not evidence of superiority on operational data.

As at 27 August 2026, manual and human-review evaluation is a no-go because ethics, data, and participant authority are unverified. If authorisation remains absent at protocol freeze, the manual comparison will be reported as unanswered, the primary research question as only partially answered, and synthetic evidence will not be substituted. The public company/model path also remains live-unrun; official live adapters, a frozen public benchmark, provider-retention and cost evidence, and production/accessibility validation are outstanding. The contribution is therefore an auditable artefact and evaluation protocol, limited to controlled technical feasibility rather than reduced real-world effort, improved investment decisions, exhaustive web coverage, or production readiness.
