## Paragraph 1: bounded comparative gap, not novelty

- `kaplan2016vcdata`, PDF pp. 3--7: private venture-capital data have coverage, definition,
  non-reporting and backfill limitations.
- `buneman2001provenance`, PDF pp. 1--3, and `gao2023alce`, PDF pp. 1, 3--4 and 10: data origin,
  citation support and truth are distinct questions.
- `visible2026investors`, captured PDF pp. 1--4; `zapflow2026portfolio`, captured PDF pp. 1--4; and
  `affinity2026portfolio`, captured PDF pp. 1--3: vendor pages describe different portfolio,
  investment-operation and relationship-reporting categories without comparative validation.

Safe synthesis: the gap is a bounded evaluation of identity, missingness, time, provenance,
verification and human-authority controls under shared early-stage inputs. It is not a claim that
the components or products are absent, novel or inferior.

## Paragraph 2: RQ1 derivation

- `thorne2026funding`, PDF pp. 1--6, and `kaplan2016vcdata`, PDF pp. 3--7: company/funding records can
  be incomplete, inconsistently defined and difficult to link.
- `gao2023rarr`, PDF pp. 1--5: attribution does not guarantee truth or that retrieved evidence is
  correctly used.
- `nikiforova2020quality`, PDF pp. 1--3 and 15--17, and `pineau2021reproducibility`, PDF pp. 1--5 and
  9--13: use-specific dimensions, explicit procedures and repeat evidence are necessary.

Safe synthesis: RQ1 evaluates transformation meaning, support, provenance and repeat reliability. A
generated report alone does not answer it.

## Paragraph 3: RQ2 derivation

- `guo2024multiagent`, PDF pp. 3--5 and 10--11, and `cemri2025masfailures`, PDF pp. 1--3: role
  decomposition creates coordination, propagation, design and verification failure possibilities.
- `peffers2007dsrm`, PDF pp. 4 and 16--18, and `pineau2021reproducibility`, PDF pp. 1--5 and 9--13:
  evaluation must isolate the condition and report quality plus resource measures.

Safe synthesis: compare equal-input conditions with and without a separately recorded verifier, while
retaining a single-process same-gate alternative. Do not attribute an effect to agent names.

## Paragraph 4: RQ3 derivation

- `amershi2019guidelines`, PDF pp. 2--8, and `bucinca2021forcing`, PDF pp. 1--4 and 16--18: human
  correction and authority are plausible controls, but over-reliance, usability and group effects
  remain empirical questions.
- `visible2026investors`, captured PDF pp. 1--4, and `zapflow2026portfolio`, captured PDF pp. 1--4:
  vendor pages describe reporting workflows without independently measuring reviewer effort or
  decision quality.

Safe synthesis: RQ3 requires an authorised matched participant study of manual, pre-review and
reviewed outputs. An implemented approval screen is not human-benefit evidence.

## Paragraph 5: bounded public-web research remains separate

- `openai2026websearch`, PDF pp. 1--7 and 10--12: the captured documentation establishes search,
  citation, source-list and filtering capabilities, not prototype performance.
- `greshake2023indirect`, PDF pp. 1 and 3--5, and `gao2023alce`, PDF pp. 3--4 and 10: searched content
  can be hostile and visible citations can remain unsupported or incomplete.
- `pineau2021reproducibility`, PDF pp. 1--4: unrun or unfrozen work cannot be reported as a result.

Safe synthesis: the official-adapter-only versus LLM-assisted comparison remains a held engineering
case pending authority, protocol amendment and benchmark freeze. It is not an extra research question.
