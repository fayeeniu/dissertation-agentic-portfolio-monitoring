\chapter{Ethics, Governance and Limitations}
\section{Data classification, privacy and purpose limits}

At intake, evidence is marked restricted, internal, public or synthetic. Restricted portfolio and
internal operating data stay local and cannot reach an external model. External processing of public
or synthetic material requires explicit selection and a stated case purpose. Holding less data lowers
the exposure, but the marking depends on a person getting it right, and it settles neither the legal
basis for this work nor the university's approval of it \citep{cddo2023genai,nist2023airmf}.

Local source copies and exports are checksum-linked, written once where required, kept out of Git and
privately permissioned, and personal contact details are removed before an accepted public page is
stored. Two boundaries matter here. A checksum shows that a file has changed; it neither anonymises
nor encrypts the contents. And information being publicly available does not settle whether this
project may use it, because privacy, licence and purpose are separate questions from access
\citep{gebru2021datasheets,cddo2023genai}.

Work with real restricted data or with participants stays held until an approved data-management and
ethics plan names who controls the data and who owns purpose, access, retention, deletion and incident
response. No retention period has been invented in the meantime. What the prototype demonstrates is a
local purpose limit, which is a design property rather than a data-protection judgement
\citep{nist2023airmf,cddo2023genai}.
\section{External-model processing and retention}

The optional external route sends only approved public or synthetic company-level material and a
reference that does not expose evidence. It requires open collection/model gates, reviewed identity,
named reviewer, approved route and a runtime key. Requests use \texttt{store=False}, strict schemas and
fixed limits. Restricted, internal or personal-contact content is prohibited, and model output cannot
approve or export a report \citep{cddo2023genai,nist2023airmf}.

Live external-model requests did occur during development, and this section records them. On
26 August 2026, between 20:03 and 20:17 UTC, five bounded smoke runs sent six requests to the OpenAI
Responses structured-extraction provider. Each run passed one checksum-pinned synthetic evidence item
from the local fixture set through the ordinary extraction stage. No restricted, internal or
real-company material was sent, and the gates above were in force. Three requests succeeded on model
\texttt{gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17}, each recording 449 input tokens, 79 output tokens and between 1.4
and 1.9 seconds. Three failed: one was refused by the provider, and two produced output that the local
schema check rejected, the second after escalating to \texttt{gpt-5.4-2026-03-05}
\citep{cddo2023genai,nist2023airmf}.

These runs are development evidence about whether the route works. They are not a result. They sit
outside the dated evaluation snapshot in Chapter~5, they measure no accuracy, latency or cost, and six
requests on one synthetic item cannot describe provider behaviour. What the project can observe also
stops at its own boundary: the runs confirm what was sent and what came back, but nothing about what
the provider then retained. OpenAI separates saved application state from abuse-monitoring retention,
and \texttt{store=False} is not Zero Data Retention, so deletion remains unverified. Before any use
with real company material, the account settings, eligibility, current terms and data location need
checking, and the confirming evidence needs recording
\citep{openai2026datacontrols,cddo2023genai}.
\section{Security and failure threats}

Public pages may contain hidden instructions, misleading identities or malformed content. The route
treats downloaded text as data, limits model tools, rejects instruction-like passages and demands
exact support before it accepts a claim. The tests cover chosen risks; the controls cannot spot every
hostile form, nor promise that a plausible supported statement is harmless
\citep{greshake2023indirect,autio2024genai}.

Collection checks the protocol, embedded credentials, ports, network addresses, redirects, robots
rules, content type, elapsed time and decompressed size, and connects to a verified public address
while still checking the certificate hostname. Controlled tests covering internal-service access,
address switching, redirects, content type and size all passed. Each test confirms that one specific
input was refused, which is a narrower thing than a security assessment
\citep{autio2024genai,nist2023airmf}.

Budgets, bounded retries, cancellation, task fingerprints, checksums and staged export together limit
runaway cost, stale completion, altered files and partial writes. A forced stop can still leave work
that needs deliberate recovery, and the dependencies and sources underneath can change without notice
\citep{pineau2021reproducibility,nist2023airmf}.
\section{Investment and public-person boundaries}

The report links company events to evidence; it does not advise buying or selling, rank companies,
create speculative valuations or assess suitability. Funding, awards, contracts, filings and attention
are contextual and do not themselves cause or measure quality or future performance. Because financial
information can influence decisions, external use requires separate review of purpose, audience and
presentation \citep{fca2024promotions,estrin2024digital}.

The research concerns companies, not individuals. Captured contact details are removed; director,
employee, investor or journalist profiles, inferred sensitive traits and reputation scores are
excluded. A named public statement may support a relevant company claim but cannot become a judgement
about that person. Human approval cannot expand these limits \citep{cddo2023genai,nist2023airmf}.
\section{Research and operational limitations}

This section is the single place where the study's limits are stated in full. Earlier chapters
therefore report what was built and observed without repeating these boundaries, and a reader who
wants the honest edge of every claim in this dissertation should read this section rather than hunt for
caveats attached to individual results. Stating validity limits in one place, rather than scattering
them, follows both design-science reporting practice and risk-management guidance that ties a claim to
its tested context \citep{hevner2004design,nist2023airmf}.

The principal evidential limit is D0 itself. Its fourteen cases are fictional, and they were written
by the same person who wrote the rules they test, so the verifier difference may partly reflect that
shared design rather than an independent property of the system. Behavioural test suites are built to
expose specific known failures; passing them shows that the targeted behaviour is present, not that
the system is sound in ways the suite never probes \citep{ribeiro2020checklist}. Each error category
in D0 also has only one case, so no rate, distribution or confidence interval can be derived. Nothing
in this dissertation estimates accuracy on real companies, parity with manual work, reviewer benefit
or population performance \citep{kapoor2023leakage,pineau2021reproducibility}.

Several planned comparisons produced no observations and are absent rather than negative. The manual
baseline, the participant-review condition, the live public-web study and the managed-platform
comparison all required authority, frozen references or recorded human effort that was not available.
Engineering evidence is bounded in the same way: 286 passing tests and 85.58 per cent statement
coverage describe exercised local paths using fictional data and fake providers. Unit and end-to-end
tests remain valuable, but in a changing world they are not sufficient evidence that a system works as
intended \citep{sculley2015debt,pineau2021reproducibility}. Construction and passing tests are
therefore treated throughout as artefact evidence, never as effectiveness
\citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}.

Anything outside the repository can move. Sources, licences, data formats, publisher pages, search
ordering, model behaviour and provider policy all change independently of this code, and a checksum
detects that a saved file has altered without preserving access to the original or its meaning. Fixed
rules repeat consistently, which is a strength until a rule is wrong: manually set thresholds and
frozen mappings become invalid as the world they encode moves on, and each one carries an ongoing
maintenance cost \citep{sculley2015debt,gebru2021datasheets}. The researcher designed the system,
wrote the cases and performed the scoring and interpretation, which is a clear confirmation risk. Any
final evaluation therefore needs independently constructed references and a freeze agreed before the
data are seen \citep{nist2023airmf}.

Operationally the prototype is local, single-user, serial and SQLite-based. It has no production
authentication, tenant separation, egress isolation, central monitoring, backup regime, service target
or tested disaster recovery, and its accessibility was checked visually rather than with assistive
technology or an independent assessor. The dated snapshot also retains five formatting failures and 22
deprecation warnings, and changes made after that snapshot are unevaluated. None of this cancels the
controlled D0 result, but together these limits rule out any claim about usability, resilience,
maintainability or production readiness. The end of Section~6.7 lists the testing that would be
needed to close these gaps, and Table~\ref{tab:gov-residual-risk}, in
Appendix~\ref{app:supplementary-exhibits}, summarises where authority and residual risk still sit
\citep{hevner2004design,amershi2019guidelines}.
