\chapter{The Artefact: the life of a claim}
\section{Requirements and trust boundary}

This chapter follows one claim from intake to approved export. The release is a local, single-user
research prototype. It listens only on the machine that runs it and refuses any client that is not
local. A bounded company-research route may start from a Companies House number alone, but a named
person must review that number before research can begin. Incorporation is not proof that the company
trades \citep{galanakis2026chrt,hardman2023small}.

Every item is labelled public, internal, restricted or synthetic. External-model processing is off by
default and, when switched on, accepts only public or synthetic evidence. The client sends
\texttt{store=False} with every request, which controls saved application state but not
abuse-monitoring retention \citep{gebru2021datasheets,openai2026datacontrols}. Export is blocked until
the current version is approved. Table~\ref{tab:sys-requirements-compact} states the trust boundary.
The full requirement matrix is Table~\ref{tab:sys-requirements-trust-boundary} in
Appendix~\ref{app:requirements-traceability} \citep{fca2024promotions,nist2023airmf}.

\section{Architecture in one page}

The artefact runs as two local processes. Next.js renders the operator dashboard. Its server-side
proxy calls a private FastAPI service, so the browser never reaches the API directly. SQLite holds
identity, workflow, claim, review and audit records. Imported bytes and source captures are written
once into local directories that Git ignores, verified by checksum and recorded by path
\citep{pineau2021reproducibility,gebru2021datasheets}. Docker Compose mirrors that two-process shape
in pinned images. Figure~\ref{fig:sys-architecture-deployment-boundary} maps the request and data
path \citep{hevner2004design,nist2023airmf}. The canonical data model, including the separate
company-research track, is Figure~\ref{fig:sys-canonical-data-provenance-model} in
Appendix~\ref{app:requirements-traceability}. Startup migrates the database to the declared head
rather than creating tables directly, so the schema always has a stated version. Alembic revisions
0001--0009 reproduce the evaluated schema; revision 0010 was added after the dated snapshot, which
Chapter~5 records at 0009. Tests compare the migrated schema against the one the code expects
\citep{pineau2021reproducibility,gebru2021datasheets}.

\section{Stages 1--3: intake, identity and capture}

The workflow is a fixed state machine with eight stages: plan, resolve identity, collect evidence,
extract values, normalise them, verify claims, compose a report and stop at human review. Each stage
leaves a persisted run record holding input and output hashes, status, timing and attempts. A failure
stops the stages that would have followed it \citep{pineau2021reproducibility,hevner2004design}.

Identity is stage 2. The portfolio-import route accepts XLSX, CSV and JSON, hashes the original bytes
and writes a create-once snapshot. Every company row passes through the same identity resolver. A
conflicting identifier becomes a counted hold rather than a silent merge. A well-formed Companies
House number creates an exact identifier and a held case. It is not confirmed against the register,
because valid shape is not proof of existence. Names may suggest a candidate. They never merge
companies \citep{thorne2026funding,surak2026gateways}. Identity becomes ready only after a named actor
accepts the exact identifier with a substantive reason. Figure~\ref{fig:sys-legal-identity-decision-flow},
in Appendix~\ref{app:requirements-traceability}, maps these gates.

Capture is stage 3. Collection stores run-linked evidence and its time decision before extraction.
Only temporally eligible, trusted public evidence reaches later stages. Untrusted material is counted
and bypassed. Connector snapshots record source and version, reviewed identifier, cutoff, locator,
media type, checksum, retrieval and publication times, and classification
\citep{pineau2021reproducibility,gebru2021datasheets}. Direct live Companies House and UKRI retrieval
remain held at gate G2. Offline replay of those adapters is implementation evidence, recorded in
Appendix~\ref{app:requirements-traceability}.

Time is judged relative to the claim, not to the moment of collection. Evidence is ineligible if it
carries no recorded publication or availability time, was published after the reporting cut-off,
takes effect in the future, or had expired before the period began. When it was retrieved is tracked
separately. A cumulative figure must span exactly from the stored programme start to the cut-off.
UKRI funding binds only when every award in the window carries a finite, explicit sterling amount,
and otherwise leaves a missing state with a diagnostic rather than an inferred zero
\citep{hardman2023small,kapoor2023leakage,nikiforova2020quality}. The source layer is a registry of
declared capabilities rather than a crawler. Every request names the source, company, reviewed
identifier, cut-off, fact keys and mode, and the registry refuses it before any adapter runs if the
capability is unsupported or the identity is unresolved. A refusal of this kind is a decision not to
look, recorded differently from a publisher having no record
\citep{gebru2021datasheets,pineau2021reproducibility}. Two deterministic adapters are usable through
synthetic offline replay: Companies House (manifest 1.4.0) matches an exact number, and UKRI
(manifest 1.3.0) matches an exact organisation identifier and selects the most recent correction
available at the cut-off \citep{galanakis2026chrt,hardman2023small}.

\section{Stages 4--5: extraction and normalisation}

Stage 4 extracts a candidate value. Structured evidence is extracted with fixed, rule-based code. A
strict schema binds company, metric, period, value and exact evidence locator. Unstructured public or
synthetic evidence may use an optional model only to suggest a claim that still links to an exact
passage \citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr}. Existing extractions are reused. Every provider attempt is
stored, including the failed ones.

Stage 5 normalises type, unit, currency and missing state against a versioned catalogue. Blank,
explicit zero, none stated, not applicable, not reported, not found publicly, source unavailable and
invalid are the eight states used in the narrative. Distinguishing them is what allows a later
reader to see why a figure is absent, and what allows stage 6 to treat an observed zero as sayable
rather than as a blank to be dropped
\citep{nikiforova2020quality,gebru2021datasheets,bradley2024synfintabs}. The implemented type
system also records observed, filing-not-due, dormant and not-required. Many of these semantic
states are stored as strings or JSON and enforced by application code rather than by the
database, so the schema alone cannot guarantee they are used consistently. The catalogue version
is part of the evaluand: a later change of missing-state rules is a new configuration, not a
silent improvement of the same one \citep{pineau2021reproducibility,mitchell2019modelcards}.

\section{Stage 6: separate verification}

Verification is the stage that decides what may be said. Before a claim is constructed, the workflow
checks sourceability, provenance, period, trust, value and currency, then writes a verification
record beside the claim. The verifier is a pure function returning one of five states: supported,
contradicted, insufficient evidence, stale, or rejected as untrusted. A conflict between two current
public sources cannot be averaged away. The verifier runs separately from the extractor and the
composer and cannot rewrite their records, so a claim can be refused but not quietly repaired
\citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr,nist2023airmf}. This is separation of duties inside one program, not
independent judgement by a second party.

Figure~\ref{fig:sys-fixed-workflow-verification-state-machine} is the anchor for that contract. A
single program could apply the identical support rule. The D0 comparison isolates the rule and its
authority over composition, not the number of roles
\citep{guo2024multiagent,wu2024autogen,peffers2007dsrm}. Claims persist normalised values and link
many-to-many with evidence items; each evidence item carries publisher, locator, times, checksum,
connector version, classification and trust or time flags. A separate verification stage records
supported, contradicted, insufficient, stale or untrusted outcomes without rewriting the source
\citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr}. Portfolio reports can export these links without raw evidence. Where
two sources disagree, the company-research track groups the statements as contradiction candidates
for a named person to resolve. No live company has been run through that path
\citep{yue2023attrscore,nist2023airmf}.

\section{Stages 7--8: composition, approval and export}

Composition places supported claims in the narrative and exposes other states as exceptions. It
hashes the current, versioned report and moves it to pending review. A named reviewer may approve,
reject or request an edit. Each decision records actor, reason and report version. Editing creates a
new section version, recalculates the content hash and revokes approval. Export remains unavailable
until current approval \citep{amershi2019guidelines,bucinca2021forcing,pineau2021reproducibility}.
Files are staged below the local export root, bound to a versioned manifest and content hash, then
re-verified before reuse. The system writes files to a folder. It does not publish.

Company-research profiles use a separate, narrower gate. Before a named approve or reject decision,
the service rechecks the profile hash, pinned run contract and completed composition task; stale
locks, altered content and unfinished recovery are refused. Contradiction candidates and coverage
gaps stay inside the dossier the reviewer sees. Only approved profiles can be downloaded, as JSON or
printable HTML \citep{gao2023alce,nist2023airmf}. Reports are versioned by appending rather than
overwriting, so an edit retires the current section instead of erasing it. Approval is bound to an
exact version of exact content: change the content and the approval falls away
\citep{pineau2021reproducibility,amershi2019guidelines}.

\section{Two claims traced end to end}
\label{sec:claim-journey}

Two D0 cases show what these stages do to a single claim. Both use fictional companies, a pinned
fixture and the same candidate-preparation route. C1 emits eligible cleaned values. C2 admits a claim
only when the separate verifier marks it supported. Chapter~5 reports the aggregate rates. This
section shows the decisions themselves \citep{gao2023alce,bradley2024synfintabs}.

The first claim is an observed zero: jobs created equals 0. The submitted figure is zero. The stored
evidence also says zero. The two agree, so the verifier marks the claim supported and it appears in
the report with its source attached. The obvious shortcut would be to treat a zero as an empty box
and quietly drop it. Doing so would remove a real and possibly unwelcome fact. The eight narrative
missing states exist so that an observed nothing remains sayable
\citep{bradley2024synfintabs,gebru2021datasheets}.

The second claim is a contradiction: grant funding of \pounds100 against register evidence of
\pounds200 for the same period. The two figures cannot both be right, and nothing in the evidence
establishes which is. The verifier marks the claim contradicted. The report omits it. The disagreement
is kept on record for a named person. The system does not choose the larger figure, the newer one or
an average, because any of those would manufacture a number that no source states
\citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr,yue2023attrscore}. For a reader, the useful contrast is that C1
emitted this claim while C2 refused admission of it. Table~\ref{tab:eval-d0-case-examples} in
Chapter~5 records both outcomes against the fixture locators.

Table~\ref{tab:sys-claim-journey} walks both rows through the eight stages: intake, identity,
capture, extract, normalise, verify, compose, and review or export. The locators are
\texttt{submission://synthetic\#B2} for the observed zero and \texttt{fixture://conflict} for the
grant pair. The weekly monitoring load in Section~\ref{sec:reporting-problem} is why those locators
have to survive: an untraced figure spends attention without leaving a defensible record
\citep{britishbusinessbank2025equity,gompers2016vcdecisions}. The walk is therefore a reading
guide for those two fixture rows, not a second scored evaluation of the same fixture.

At intake the operator records the candidate as typed. The observed-zero row arrives as jobs
created equals 0. The conflict row arrives as grant funding of \pounds100. Neither figure is yet
a report sentence. Identity then binds each row to one fictional legal company. These two D0 rows
do not exercise an identity hold. The hold is the same contract that later keeps two similarly
named companies apart, and it is evidenced by engineering tests rather than by these fixture
identifiers. Capture stores the supporting bytes before anyone extracts a value. The zero is
stored at \texttt{submission://synthetic\#B2}. The register figure of \pounds200 is stored at
\texttt{fixture://conflict}. If those locators were missing, a later reader could not reopen the
same evidence. Stages 1 to 3 for these two rows are therefore the workflow contract. They are
evidenced by engineering tests, not by a full pipeline replay of the fixture through the
dashboard \citep{gebru2021datasheets,pineau2021reproducibility,nikiforova2020quality}.

Extract binds company, metric, period, value and exact locator. For the first row the candidate
remains 0. For the second it remains \pounds100. Normalise is where the catalogue earns its keep.
The zero is typed as an explicit zero rather than as a blank, none stated or not reported, so it
stays sayable. The grant pair stays as two current sterling amounts for the same period. Nothing
is converted, averaged or replaced by the newer figure. Verify then writes the admission
decision. The two zeros agree, so the state is supported. The two grant figures disagree, so the
state is contradicted. D0 scores that admission decision, which is the normalise-plus-verify
pair. It does not score whether an operator liked the screen
\citep{gao2023alce,bradley2024synfintabs,yue2023attrscore}.

Compose is allowed to write only what verify admitted. C2 therefore emits the observed zero with
its source record and withholds the grant claim, keeping the disagreement for a named person. C1
emitted the contradicted grant because it had no separate refusal. Review and export then bind a
name, a reason and a content hash. An edit drops the previous approval. Export remains a local
file write, not a publication. Stages 7 and 8 for these two rows are again the workflow
contract, evidenced by engineering tests rather than by a scored dashboard replay of the
fixture. That is why the table can name an approval lock without claiming that anyone sat in
front of the screen for these cases
\citep{amershi2019guidelines,bucinca2021forcing,pineau2021reproducibility}.
D0 therefore scores the admission decision, which is the normalise-plus-verify pair, and it
leaves stages 1 to 3 and 7 to 8 as a workflow contract. Engineering tests show that those
stages persist identity, capture, review and export records. They do not replay these two
fixture rows through every operator screen. That split is why the table can be read as a
life of a claim without being mistaken for a usability result or a live-source run
\citep{hevner2004design,pineau2021reproducibility}.

Two further D0 refusals complete the picture of what the gate is for. A stale-period case carries a
matching awards figure, but only for a prior period; the verifier marks it stale and the report omits
it while keeping the old evidence visible. A prompt-injection case carries a matching partnership
count inside a fixture record marked untrusted; the verifier rejects it as untrusted and no
instruction is followed \citep{greshake2023indirect,gao2023rarr}. In both cases C1 emitted the claim
and C2 refused admission. The six refused claims are the whole of the measured difference between
the conditions, and they are silences rather than corrections
\citep{gao2023alce,huang2023hallucination}. A later reader can therefore ask not only what the
report said but which candidates were refused and why, which is the inspectability RQ1 requires and
the admission change RQ2 measures \citep{pineau2021reproducibility,hevner2004design}.

\section{Built but not evaluated}

Table~\ref{tab:meth-evidence-scope}. The public-web funnel is built and tested with fake

controls themselves are described in Appendix~\ref{app:requirements-traceability}
