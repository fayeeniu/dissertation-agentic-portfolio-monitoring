\chapter{Introduction}
\section{Early-stage portfolio reporting and company intelligence}

An investor holding stakes in a dozen young companies has to say, in writing and on a date, how each
one is doing. The awkward part is not writing the summary but standing behind it: a figure that came
from a slide deck, a funding round that was announced but never completed, or an accounts filing that
had not yet been made can each turn a confident sentence into one that cannot be defended when someone
asks where it came from. The difficulty is structural rather than careless, because early-stage
records are themselves incomplete and delayed \citep{hardman2023small}, and a statement can carry a
citation and still not be supported by it \citep{gao2023alce}. That question -- where did this claim
come from, and does the source actually say it -- is the problem this dissertation addresses.

The verified project charter describes three reporting activities: someone gathers company
information, someone turns it into an executive summary, and someone decides whether that summary
may be released. Table~\ref{tab:intro-business-process} sets out who does what. These are jobs to be
done rather than assumed job titles. The legal company and the reporting date must both be clear,
because being incorporated does not prove that a business is trading, and recent records may be
missing simply because a filing is not yet due \citep{galanakis2026chrt,hardman2023small}.

Evidence about a company's development comes from dated events rather than one complete profile.
Crowdfunding results vary by pitch, platform, country, sector, region and time. Published samples may
also include only successful pitches \citep{estrin2024digital}. Research on repeat funding uses the
campaign round as its unit and reports links with investor numbers, targets, equity offered and
overfunding on one platform \citep{wasti2024successive}. UK funding data can connect opportunities,
applications, decisions, organisations, projects and outcomes, but its coverage and links remain
incomplete \citep{thorne2026funding}. A report should keep each event tied to its date and source. It
should not turn these observations into a score that claims to measure company quality.

These limits show that reliable reporting starts with the data, not with well-written text. A company
register may be suitable for one task but not another. Legal identity and address fields are more
available than management, financial or contact information \citep{krasikov2020ready}. Even required
Companies House fields can be empty, so quality rules must be clear, linked to the intended use and
checked in code \citep{nikiforova2020quality}. Extracting financial tables can also introduce errors
through optical character recognition (OCR), repeated values, cell positions and missing negative
signs \citep{bradley2024synfintabs}. The system must record the source, date, location and check result
for each important statement. It must also show when evidence is missing or unclear.

\section{Problem definition and motivation}

The main data problem is to match different records to the correct legal company and to use only
information that was available at the relevant date. Research that joined several registers used
normalised names, alternative country names, approximate matching and manual review, but some records
still could not be matched \citep{surak2026gateways}. Companies House data can contain empty required
fields and inconsistent country names \citep{nikiforova2020quality}. Newer companies may also have no
accounts because the filing date has not arrived \citep{hardman2023small}. The prototype therefore
waits until the company number and legal identity are clear. It keeps different types of missing data
instead of changing blanks to zeros, and records when information first became available.

Text can sound convincing and still be wrong, even when it includes citations. Research on cited
answers separates factual correctness from citation precision and recall. A citation marker alone
does not guarantee any of these qualities \citep{gao2023alce}. A statement may also match a source that
is itself wrong \citep{gao2023rarr}. Public web pages can contain hidden instructions that try to
change a model's summary, source choice or tool actions \citep{greshake2023indirect}. For this reason,
web search is used only to find possible sources. Each report claim must point to a saved source and
an exact page or passage, and a separate check must confirm the support. Retrieved text is always
treated as untrusted data.

Human approval creates a release boundary, but it does not guarantee a better result. General guidance for human-AI
systems recommends explaining what the system can do, showing uncertainty and allowing people to
correct or dismiss its output \citep{amershi2019guidelines}. In one controlled task, some participants
followed incorrect AI advice. Prompts that made them stop and think reduced this problem, but they also
affected preference, trust and different participant groups in different ways
\citep{bucinca2021forcing}. The prototype shows conflicts, missing evidence, limits and exact source
locations, and it requires a named reviewer before export. The evaluation tests the evidence and
verification mechanisms, while human outcomes remain a future pilot question.

\section{Research aim}

The aim of this dissertation is to design, implement and evaluate an evidence-first, role-separated
workflow for early-stage portfolio reporting. The study examines how the prototype preserves evidence
from intake to a reviewable report and how a separate verification stage changes claim admission in
controlled cases. This follows design-science research, which links a practical problem to the
development and evaluation of an artefact \citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}.

\section{Research questions}
\label{sec:research-questions}

\subsection*{Primary research question}

\textit{How can an evidence-first, role-separated workflow produce traceable early-stage portfolio
reporting claims, and what effect does a separate verification stage have on claim admission in
controlled cases?} The question concerns the implemented workflow rather than one model, agent label
or interface. It is answered by combining RQ1 and RQ2 with the executed engineering and D0 evidence,
as required in design-science evaluation \citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}.

\subsection*{RQ1: Input-to-claim accuracy and reliability}

\textit{How does the implemented workflow preserve company identity, reporting time, missing states,
source provenance and approval boundaries from input to report?} RQ1 is answered with implementation
records, engineering tests and labelled D0 cases. The assessment covers exact identity holds, value and
period semantics, source-record completeness, claim-to-evidence links and repeated outcomes. These
measures keep data quality tied to the reporting task and keep factual correctness separate from
citation support \citep{nikiforova2020quality,gao2023alce}.

\subsection*{RQ2: Effect of separate verification}

\textit{What effect does a separately recorded verification stage have on supported-claim precision,
unsupported claims, conflict handling, source-record completeness and repeatability in D0?} RQ2 uses
a paired comparison between C1, which has no separate verifier, and C2, which adds verification.
Both conditions receive the same fictional cases and proposed values. This avoids treating a citation
link or fluent statement as proof that a claim is supported \citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr}.

\subsection*{Scope narrowing}

The original contract also proposed manual C0, named-review C3, live-web and managed-platform
comparisons. They require authorised participants, frozen references, live access and recorded time,
cost or review observations. Those observations were not available, so these comparisons are not
active research questions. Human control may support correction and accountability, but it may also
create delay or over-reliance \citep{amershi2019guidelines,bucinca2021forcing}. Chapter~7 records this
limitation once, and Chapters~6 and~8 specify the future pilot needed to test it.

\subsection*{Research objectives and evaluation plan}

Four objectives connect the questions to the study. O1 defines shared rules for legal identity,
dates, missing data and source records. O2 implements those rules in a workflow that stops when
required evidence is missing or invalid. O3 evaluates the implemented controls and the C1/C2 verifier
difference using the fixed D0 fixture. O4 distinguishes supported, unsupported, conflicting,
unavailable and missing evidence in every result. Together, these objectives connect the artefact to
observable evaluation criteria without treating successful execution as proof of practical benefit
\citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}.

\section{Design alternatives and why roles were separated}
\label{sec:design-alternatives}

A reasonable reader will ask why this problem needs several cooperating components at all. Simpler
designs exist, three of them are genuinely capable, and the case for role separation is narrower than
the phrase ``multi-agent'' usually implies. Surveys of such systems show that role labels and
communication patterns vary widely and carry no guaranteed benefit \citep{guo2024multiagent}, while
design-science practice requires an artefact's design choices to be justified against alternatives
\citep{hevner2004design}. This section therefore states the case plainly, including what it does not
claim.

The first alternative is a single model call: give one language model the evidence and the question,
and take its answer. This is the weakest option, and not because such models write badly. The
difficulty is structural. The component that writes the claim is also the component that decides the
claim is supported, so there is no position from which the claim can be refused. Work on cited
answers shows why that matters: whether a statement is factually right, whether its citation points
to the right place, and whether the cited passage actually supports it are three separate properties,
and a citation marker on its own guarantees none of them \citep{gao2023alce}. A statement can also be
faithful to a source that is itself wrong \citep{gao2023rarr}. If the writer is the only judge, those
distinctions cannot be recorded, because nothing in the design is asking the question.

The second alternative is a deterministic script with no model at all, and here the concession is
real: for structured register fields, a script is sufficient, and this project uses exactly that. The
identity, period and missing-value rules in Chapter~4 are ordinary code, not model output, because
fixed rules are the more reliable instrument when the input has a schema \citep{nikiforova2020quality}.
A script becomes insufficient only where the evidence is prose: an accounts commentary or a funding
announcement carries the figure in narrative form, and extracting it involves interpretation that a
schema cannot express. Table extraction alone introduces character-recognition errors, repeated
values, misplaced cells and dropped negative signs \citep{bradley2024synfintabs}. The honest position
is therefore a division of labour, with fixed rules wherever a rule will do and language models only
where meaning must be read from text.

The third alternative is a plain retrieval-then-generation pipeline, which is the strongest competitor
and the one closest to standard practice. Retrieving real passages before writing does reduce
invention, and this project retrieves too. The limitation is again about authority rather than
quality: in that arrangement the generator remains the last component to touch the claim, so support
is asserted by the same step that composes the text, and surveys of retrieval pipelines note that
retrieved passages may be irrelevant or unsupported \citep{gao2023ragsurvey}. Retrieved web content is
also untrusted input, because a page can carry instructions aimed at changing a model's summary,
source choice or tool use \citep{greshake2023indirect}. Retrieval improves the evidence available; it
does not create a stage that is allowed to say no.

What separation buys, then, is narrow and structural. The extractor cannot admit its own output, the
verifier can refuse a claim but cannot rewrite it to pass, the composer can use only admitted claims,
and only a named person can approve a release. These are constraints on authority, demonstrable by
inspection rather than inferred from a score. The cost is equally real: more state, more hand-offs and
more to maintain, and analysis of multi-agent traces shows that added roles introduce coordination and
verification failures of their own \citep{cemri2025masfailures,sculley2015debt}. Managed platforms can
host such roles through configuration rather than code, which Chapter~2 examines as a serious
alternative \citep{dibia2024autogenstudio}.

This leads to a claim deliberately smaller than the design might suggest, and it is worth stating
before any result appears. A single program could apply the identical support rule and reach the
identical decisions; the D0 comparison in Chapter~5 isolates that rule and its authority over
composition, not the number of components. Where Chapters~4 and~6 concede this, they state the
argument's actual shape rather than retreat from it. What is defended here is that separating who may
propose, judge and approve makes the reasons for a decision recoverable -- a property of the
arrangement, not a measured gain in accuracy
\citep{guo2024multiagent,wu2024autogen,hevner2004design}.

\section{Scope, contribution, and exclusions}

The system is a local, single-user research prototype rather than a deployed service, reachable only
from the same computer. Stating its purpose, user and operating limits matters because risk depends on
the setting in which a system is used \citep{nist2023airmf}. Public company information may be sent to
an external model only after the required checks, while restricted or internal material stays local
and is never sent to public generative-AI tools \citep{cddo2023genai}.

The contribution has three parts: an evidence-first prototype, an executed controlled evaluation and
a reporting method that traces claims to sources. It combines design with tests against stated
objectives, without treating implementation as proof of operational usefulness
\citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}. It also keeps factual correctness separate from citation
support, so each sourced claim still needs a page or passage check \citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr}.
The public-web research route provides additional implementation and adversarial-test evidence, but
it is not used to answer the active questions.

The project excludes buy or sell recommendations, speculative valuations, profiles of people,
automatic publication and claims that the system is ready for remote, multi-user or production use.
Financial communications can affect investment decisions and may create further duties. This supports
a cautious scope, not a claim of legal compliance \citep{fca2024promotions}. Public web content remains
untrusted because hidden prompt injection can change model output or tool actions
\citep{greshake2023indirect}. A named person must approve a report before export, which keeps human
responsibility clear \citep{cddo2023genai}. These controls define the prototype's authority boundary;
their wider limits are addressed in Chapter~7.
