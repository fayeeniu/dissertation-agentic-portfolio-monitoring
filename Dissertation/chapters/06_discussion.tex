\chapter{Discussion}
\section{What the workflow demonstrates}

RQ1 asks how the implemented workflow preserves identity, time, missing states, provenance and
approval boundaries from input to report. The repository and engineering checks show that these
controls exist in one local workflow. Inputs retain dataset and period identifiers; company matches
can enter an explicit hold; missing values keep typed reasons; source records retain locators and
checksums; verifier decisions are persisted; and export requires approval of the current report
version \citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}. Being able to inspect those records is the RQ1
result. It is not a claim that the same records would survive a live registry, a different schema or
a second author of the cases. Dataset documentation and reproducibility guidance both treat that
inspectability as a property of the method, not of the world the method describes
\citep{gebru2021datasheets,pineau2021reproducibility}.

D0 supplies a narrower behavioural result. Both conditions normalised all fourteen fictional cases
as expected and returned the same ordered outcomes in three repeats. C2 emitted the five supported
cases and refused admission of the remaining nine. The accepted observed-zero case in
Section~\ref{sec:claim-journey} shows why a typed zero must not be collapsed into missing data
\citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr}. Being able to trace a claim does not make its source true. What the
workflow offers is narrower: a reader can see where a value came from, what was done to it, why it
was admitted and who released it. Table~\ref{tab:disc-literature-result-alignment} sets that answer
beside the literature expectation \citep{gao2023rarr,pineau2021reproducibility}.

\section{What separate verification changed}

RQ2 compares C1 and C2 on identical D0 candidates and evidence. C1 emitted eleven claims, including
six designed to be unsupported. Its precision was 0.455 and its unsupported-claim rate was 0.545. C2
emitted five claims, all labelled as supported, giving precision of 1.000 and an unsupported-claim
rate of 0.000. Recall remained 1.000 in both conditions \citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr}.

The lower claim count is the mechanism. C2 did not improve by producing a more complete report. It
refused claim admission for conflicting values, a stale period, absent or inaccessible support, mixed
currency and untrusted content. Source-record completeness rose because the two incomplete C1 records
were among the refused claims. The observed effect is refusal, not better discovery
\citep{gao2023alce,gebru2021datasheets}. Recall stayed at 1.000 because every required supported
claim was already present in C1 and remained present in C2. The gate did not find an extra true
claim. It stopped six false ones from being said. That is why the result is described as
selective admission rather than as better discovery or as a more complete pack
\citep{gao2023rarr,peffers2007dsrm}.

C1 and C2 share one program, one catalogue and one normalisation route, so what differs is the
support rule and its authority over composition, not a persona or a count of agents. A deterministic
pipeline could apply the same rule and reach the same decisions. The finding is that an explicit,
separately recorded refusal changed what the report was allowed to say
\citep{guo2024multiagent,fu2026agents,wu2024autogen}.

No useful speed or cost comparison was produced. Both routes used local fixed rules, model cost was
zero and sub-millisecond times were truncated to zero. Role separation also adds states, hand-offs,
storage, tests and maintenance. A later pilot must measure these costs alongside false acceptance,
false rejection and reviewer work \citep{pineau2021reproducibility,hevner2004design}. D0 cannot
estimate false rejection outside its visible designed cases, so a similar gate could refuse a valid
claim in new data \citep{kapoor2023leakage,nist2023airmf}.

\section{Role separation revisited}

The selected design separates production from verification and keeps approval outside both. The
verifier can accept, refuse or contradict a candidate, but it cannot rewrite the claim to make it
pass. The composer can use only admitted claims. Section~\ref{sec:design-alternatives} already argued
that this is a constraint on authority, priced against added state and against the failure modes in
multi-agent traces \citep{cemri2025masfailures,nist2023airmf}. The prototype uses a fixed state
machine rather than an open-ended conversation to keep that cost visible
\citep{guo2024multiagent,wu2024autogen}.

The findings agree with literature that separates factual correctness, citation support and source
attribution. Exact locators and saved copies make a decision inspectable, but they do not establish
source truth or complete coverage \citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr}. The observed D0 change is
consistent with the purpose of a verification gate because the fixture was built to test that gate.
Fixed missing-state, cut-off and version rules also support repeatability. Their weakness is that a
wrong or outdated rule repeats consistently, so the rules need named ownership and re-evaluation when
sources, schemas or reporting needs change
\citep{pineau2021reproducibility,nikiforova2020quality,gebru2021datasheets}. Human approval provides
accountability for release, not automatic correctness
\citep{amershi2019guidelines,bucinca2021forcing}.

\section{What this means for an investment team}

For an analyst preparing a weekly or quarterly pack, the useful change is not a dashboard of more
figures. It is a short list of claims that survived admission, sitting beside a visible list of
refusals. The four exhibits below are the implemented operator surfaces of that local machine:
identity hold, typed missingness, retained conflict, and named approval before export
\citep{amershi2019guidelines,nist2023airmf}.

Four implemented surfaces matter in ordinary use. They are shown here as the existing
operator-facing exhibits, because a live dashboard capture of the D0 fixture rows was not
available in this tree. Figure~\ref{fig:op-local-architecture} is the machine the operator
runs. Figure~\ref{fig:op-identity-hold} is the identity hold: a Companies House number stays a
claim until a named person accepts it, so two similarly named companies are not merged by a
spelling. Figure~\ref{fig:op-verification-export} is the verification, named-approval and
export lock. Table~\ref{tab:op-missing-conflict} keeps an observed zero distinct from a blank
and groups grant funding of \pounds100 against register evidence of \pounds200 for the same
period, instead of averaging them \citep{bradley2024synfintabs,gao2023alce}. These are the
implemented operator surfaces. They are not a usability study.

The operator sees a held identifier rather than a silent merge, a typed zero rather than an
empty cell, a retained disagreement rather than a manufactured grant figure, and an export that
stays locked until the current version carries a name, a reason and a content hash. On the
identity surface the number remains a claim. On the missing-state surface ``no jobs created''
remains sayable. On the conflict surface \pounds100 stays beside \pounds200. On the approval
surface an edit drops the previous release. None of those surfaces was tested with
participants. They are the operator-facing consequence of the controls in Chapter~4, not a
measured preference or effort result \citep{amershi2019guidelines,bucinca2021forcing}.

What the team would stop doing, if the gate were trusted, is quieter than a new dashboard. It would
stop treating a missing cell as zero, stop merging two companies because their names look alike, stop
publishing the larger of two disagreeing grant figures, and stop exporting a pack whose content has
changed since the named approval. Those are reporting-control habits, not predictions about
portfolio returns \citep{fca2024promotions,nikiforova2020quality}. They still need the pilot before
anyone should change a weekly process on their account.

A managed platform could host the same roles through configuration. Grok Bot's documentation
describes persistent named Bots with jobs, tools and approval boundaries
\citep{spacexai2026grokbot,dibia2024autogenstudio}. Lower effort remains a hypothesis. The decisive
question is whether the bought platform can refuse a claim the evidence does not support.

The public-web route extends the same contract from structured replay to bounded source discovery:
web search proposes addresses, local controls decide whether a page may be collected, and a claim
still needs a saved source, an exact supporting passage and approval
\citep{openai2026websearch,gao2023alce}. Controlled tests covered reviewed identity, network checks,
request limits, write-once captures, redaction, exact passages, contradictions and approval using fake
providers and fixtures. That establishes behaviour for those cases and nothing about the open web
\citep{greshake2023indirect,nist2023airmf}. For an investment team the implication is narrow: assisted
search can lengthen the candidate list, but it cannot shorten the evidence contract.

\section{Business pilot plan}
\label{sec:business-pilot}

The proposed pilot is a time-limited evaluation of the existing portfolio XLSX import as the named
input integration, with the Next.js review interface as the downstream report surface. It has six
named parts \citep{pineau2021reproducibility,peffers2007dsrm}.

\paragraph{Integration.}
The existing XLSX import is the only in-scope input. Eligible sources, schema, cut-off and completion
rule are frozen before any paired report is started \citep{pineau2021reproducibility,peffers2007dsrm}.
Four figures still have to be chosen on site: how many portfolio companies, how many reporting
periods, what calendar window, and the smallest number of completed paired reports that would make
the comparison worth reading. This dissertation leaves those four open, because a size picked without
knowing the site's caseload would be invented rather than planned
\citep{gebru2021datasheets,nist2023airmf}.

\paragraph{Staff responsibilities.}
The pilot owner approves scope and the go/no-go decision. A reporting analyst prepares the manual
baseline and operates each reporting case. A technical operator manages the local service, versions
and failures. A reviewer checks the evidence and current report version. A data and security owner
approves data classes, source licences, storage, retention and any external-model route
\citep{amershi2019guidelines,nist2023airmf}. The analyst who prepares the manual baseline must not
also score the workflow output against that baseline after seeing both. The reviewer who sets
go/no-go thresholds must do so before paired results are visible
\citep{kapoor2023leakage,artstein2008agreement}.

\paragraph{Cost model.}
Setup effort is recorded in staff-hours for data mapping, source admission, environment
configuration, training and dry runs. For every report the team records analyst active minutes,
reviewer active minutes, elapsed time, retries, tokens, provider charges, source-licensing charges,
storage and security costs. Cost per report is allocated setup plus those items, divided by completed
reports. No saving is assumed before this matched baseline exists
\citep{pineau2021reproducibility,peffers2007dsrm}.

\paragraph{Time-saving hypotheses.}
Expected reductions in analyst active time, reviewer active time and elapsed time remain hypotheses
until the paired pilot demonstrates them on the same company-periods. A saving nobody committed to
in advance cannot later be claimed as one \citep{nikiforova2020quality,artstein2008agreement}.

\paragraph{Success metrics and go/no-go.}
Quality measures are supported-claim precision, evidence coverage, false acceptance, false rejection,
reviewer edits, completion rate and source failures. Before seeing outcomes, the pilot owner and
reviewer set the minimum acceptable quality and completion values and the maximum acceptable time,
false-acceptance, false-rejection and cost values. The recommendation is go only when those frozen
criteria are met, approval cannot be bypassed, and every exported claim has the required evidence
record \citep{peffers2007dsrm,nist2023airmf}. The run stops immediately for unclear authority or
consent, unexpected restricted or personal data, credential exposure, a source-licence or robots
breach, external transmission outside the approved route, a broken evaluation freeze,
evidence-record loss, approval bypass, uncontrolled cost or a security incident. The event, affected
cases and recovery decision are recorded \citep{cddo2023genai,nist2023airmf}.

\paragraph{Testing still required.}
An independent tester needs to attack the collection and extraction route with hostile inputs. An
accessibility assessment needs assistive technology and an assessor from outside the project. A
security assessment needs to cover remote and multi-user operation, which this prototype does not
support. A live-source study needs to run the approved official connectors against real registry and
publisher data \citep{nist2023airmf,pineau2021reproducibility}. Expected time or cost reduction
remains a hypothesis until the paired pilot demonstrates it. Results should be paired by
company-period, and failed or abandoned cases should remain in the denominator where the measure
requires them. Data-quality measures must remain tied to the intended reporting use, and reviewer
disagreement must not be treated as proof that either person is correct
\citep{nikiforova2020quality,artstein2008agreement}.

\section{What this study cannot show}
\label{sec:limitations}

This section is the only place where the study's limits are stated in full. Earlier chapters report
what was built and observed without repeating these boundaries
\citep{hevner2004design,nist2023airmf}.

The principal evidential limit is D0 itself. Its fourteen cases are fictional, and they were written
by the same person who wrote the rules they test, so the verifier difference may partly reflect that
shared design. Behavioural test suites expose specified known failures; passing them shows that the
targeted behaviour is present \citep{ribeiro2020checklist,kapoor2023leakage}. Each error category has
only one case, so no rate, distribution or confidence interval can be derived. Nothing in this
dissertation estimates accuracy on real companies, parity with manual work, reviewer benefit or
population performance.

Several planned comparisons produced no observations and are absent rather than negative. The manual
baseline, the participant-review condition, the live public-web study and the managed-platform
comparison all required authority, frozen references or recorded human effort that was not available.
Engineering evidence is bounded in the same way: 286 passing tests and 85.58~per cent statement
coverage describe exercised local paths using fictional data and fake providers
\citep{sculley2015debt,pineau2021reproducibility}.

Anything outside the repository can move. Sources, licences, data formats, search ordering, model
behaviour and provider policy all change independently of this code. Fixed rules repeat consistently,
which is a strength until a rule is wrong, and each frozen mapping carries an ongoing maintenance
cost \citep{sculley2015debt,gebru2021datasheets}. The researcher designed the system, wrote the cases
and performed the scoring. Any final evaluation therefore needs independently constructed references
and a freeze agreed before the data are seen \citep{nist2023airmf,pineau2021reproducibility}.

Operationally the prototype is local, single-user, serial and SQLite-based. It has no production
authentication, tenant separation, egress isolation, central monitoring, backup regime or tested
disaster recovery. Accessibility was checked visually rather than with assistive technology. The
dated snapshot retains five formatting failures and 22 deprecation warnings. None of this cancels
the controlled D0 result. Together these limits rule out any claim about usability, resilience,
maintainability or production readiness
\citep{hevner2004design,amershi2019guidelines}.

The study also cannot show that an investment team would accept refusal as a reporting habit.
Nothing was measured about trust, override rates or whether a visible silence is treated as
caution or as a defect. Time-saving and cost-reduction claims are out of scope for the same
reason: no paired manual hour was recorded. Vendor dashboards were reviewed as documentation,
not as measured alternatives \citep{visible2026investors,zapflow2026portfolio}. Transfer to
another legal register or another reporting cycle would require a new data contract, which is
why this section stops at that boundary and Section~\ref{sec:business-pilot} remains a plan
rather than a result
\citep{kaplan2016vcdata,britishbusinessbank2025equity}.

\section{Transferring the design elsewhere}

Moving the design to another setting is not a matter of copying code. It would need a new data
dictionary, a legal identity source, source licences, missing-value rules, a reporting cut-off, an
evidence contract, a security boundary and a named approval owner
\citep{gebru2021datasheets,nist2023airmf}. Table~\ref{tab:disc-transfer-conditions} lists those
conditions. The prototype ranks nothing, predicts nothing and advises nobody
\citep{fca2024promotions,hevner2004design}.

Everything above applies to one local prototype, fourteen designed cases and a set of controlled
security tests. Early-stage private-market evidence is unusually incomplete and sensitive to how terms
are defined, which is why statistical generalisation would need an independently selected and labelled
set rather than a larger version of D0
\citep{kaplan2016vcdata,britishbusinessbank2025equity,kapoor2023leakage}. Checksums and versions help
explain why two runs differ; they do not hold behaviour steady when the sources or models underneath
change \citep{gebru2021datasheets,nist2023airmf}. The transfer question is therefore a new
data-contract question, not a packaging question. A team that copies the code without copying the
identity source, the missing-state rules and the named approval owner has not transferred the
artefact; it has transferred a shell \citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}. The checklist in
Table~\ref{tab:disc-transfer-conditions} is therefore a list of new decisions, not a packing list.
