\chapter{Literature Review}
\section{What this review has to establish}

This review has to establish four things before the artefact is described. First, what the
reporting problem is, who does it and where time, rework and error enter the current cycle.
Second, why public early-stage evidence resists being consolidated into one pack. Third, what
makes a reporting claim defensible, as distinct from fluent or merely cited. Fourth, who is
allowed to decide: which programmed role may propose a value, which may refuse claim admission,
and which named person may release a report. Design-science reviews exist to fix those criteria
before construction is judged \citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}.

Candidate records were identified in Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, the ACM Digital Library
and the ACL Anthology. Google Scholar was used only to chase citations of already included papers.
The screen was limited to English. Agent and language-model work was taken from 2022 onwards.
Grey literature was admitted only for governance or market context and is labelled as such in
Appendix~\ref{app:literature-search}. A systematic open-literature pass screened 53 records and
retained 46 in the synthesis matrix. This chapter also uses already-admitted UK-register,
governance and vendor sources that sat outside that screen, so the dissertation's admitted
citation pool is larger than the matrix $N$. Recording both figures is required if the search
method is to be inspectable \citep{pineau2021reproducibility,gebru2021datasheets}. The appendix
records the screen. It does not make the whole bibliography a systematic review.

\section{The reporting problem: market, users, current process}
\label{sec:reporting-problem}

The market that needs these packs is large, and the monitoring load is already high. The British
Business Bank records \pounds10.8~billion of announced UK smaller-business equity investment
across 2,048 deals in 2024, while deal numbers fell by 15.1~per cent and seed- and venture-stage
activity weakened \citep{britishbusinessbank2025equity}. A survey of 885 institutional venture
capitalists reported an average of 18 hours each week working with portfolio companies, and about
60~per cent interacted with them at least weekly \citep{gompers2016vcdecisions}. Those figures
describe attention, not the effect of a reporting tool. They show why a claim that cannot be
traced is expensive: it consumes that attention without leaving a defensible record.

Five roles share the current cycle. A data contributor supplies company records. A reporting
analyst turns those records into a draft pack. A technical operator runs collection and the local
tools. A reviewer decides whether the pack may be released. A downstream user, typically an
investment-team reader, uses the released pack. The names are jobs to be done rather than assumed
job titles. Guidance on human--AI work treats the reviewer as a decision authority rather than as
a second writer \citep{amershi2019guidelines,bucinca2021forcing}. The analyst and contributor
roles exist because the underlying records are mixed, delayed and incomplete
\citep{nikiforova2020quality,hardman2023small}.

The current process is a sequence of hand-offs, and time, rework and error enter at known points
rather than as a single late failure. Collection consumes time in chasing files and public
extracts; error enters as mixed formats, uncertain identity and blanks that later readers cannot
interpret \citep{nikiforova2020quality,krasikov2020ready}. Identity resolution is the first
rework loop: similar names are joined by hand, and a silent merge is the characteristic error
\citep{surak2026gateways,thorne2026funding}. Period matching is the second: a figure from the
wrong filing window looks complete and is wrong, especially when recent accounts are not yet due
\citep{hardman2023small,galanakis2026chrt}. Drafting is where unsupported fluent sentences enter,
because a citation marker can sit beside a statement the passage does not support
\citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr}. Review is where unrecorded corrections and unclear release
authority enter; showing uncertainty does not by itself prevent over-reliance
\citep{amershi2019guidelines,bucinca2021forcing}.

What the pack may say is narrower than what the market talks about. Crowdfunding results vary by
pitch, platform, country, sector, region and time, and published samples may include only
successful pitches \citep{estrin2024digital}. Repeat-funding research uses the campaign round as
its unit \citep{wasti2024successive}. UK work on public support found later differences between
participating firms, but it also noted selection effects and did not claim that the support caused
those differences \citep{mondal2025state,thorne2026funding}. A round, grant or award can support a
dated fact. It cannot prove future success or provide a complete funding history. Financial
communications must present benefits and risks fairly, which rules out speculative valuations and
any general company-quality score \citep{fca2024promotions,mondal2025state}.

None of those five roles is measured for hours or error rate in this dissertation. The point of
naming them here is to locate where a later artefact has to intervene: identity before draft,
typed missingness before a zero is assumed, a recorded refusal before a conflict is averaged, and
a named approval before export. The current cycle can do each of those things by habit. It does
not, on the reviewed evidence, leave a complete record of them
\citep{nikiforova2020quality,gao2023alce}.

\section{Why public early-stage evidence resists consolidation}

Public records can support analysis and still refuse to become one complete profile. The UK
register literature disagrees productively about how much that register can carry. Galanakis and
colleagues treat incorporation records as a timely and broad indicator of company creation,
valuing the register for its currency \citep{galanakis2026chrt}. Hardman reaches an almost
opposite conclusion about recent data: filing rules and delays make the newest records the least
complete part of the register \citep{hardman2023small}. Krasikov and colleagues add a third
position: open access is not the same as readiness, because a register may be fully published and
still unsuitable for a given business task \citep{krasikov2020ready}. The disagreement is
resolvable. Timeliness holds for the fact of incorporation and fails for financial filings. A
report can therefore accept register identity as authoritative while treating recency of financial
records as a property to be checked per field.

Linking those records to the correct legal company is a second barrier. Funding sources without
shared identifiers need careful exact or approximate matching, and errors and gaps still remain
\citep{thorne2026funding}. Work across several registers required name normalisation, approximate
comparison and manual checks when company or country names disagreed \citep{surak2026gateways}.
Companies House identifies incorporated companies rather than all economic activity
\citep{galanakis2026chrt}. An uncertain match should stay unresolved until a named reviewer checks
it. Records with similar names must not be merged automatically.

Private-market evidence is incomplete in a different way. Kaplan and Lerner describe venture-capital
data as inconsistent, with definitional differences, missing failed firms and backfilling
\citep{kaplan2016vcdata}. The British Business Bank treats angel-investment evidence as partial
because much activity is private and reporting is patchy \citep{britishbusinessbank2025equity}.
Missingness must keep its meaning. Documentation should state what is missing, why it is missing
and what is still unknown \citep{gebru2021datasheets}. An empty financial cell does not
automatically mean zero \citep{bradley2024synfintabs}. Completeness and validity are also separate
qualities that need their own checks \citep{nikiforova2020quality}. Not found publicly does not
mean that something does not exist.

Time makes consolidation harder still. A report needs separate dates for when an event happened,
when the information became available, when it was collected and the last date allowed in the
pack. Register snapshots and filing delays show why recent facts may not have been available at
an earlier reporting date \citep{hardman2023small}. Incorporation and later business activity
happen at different times \citep{galanakis2026chrt}. Using information published after the
reporting date creates data leakage and may make an evaluation look better than it should
\citep{kapoor2023leakage}. A correction needs a new version and date. It must not be used to
support an earlier report.

Commercial products cover related but non-equivalent workflows, and setting them beside that
data-quality literature exposes the gap. Visible describes portfolio-data collection, monitoring,
dashboards and investor reporting; Zapflow describes integrated deal and portfolio records; and
Affinity describes relationship-centred activity capture and reports
\citep{visible2026investors,zapflow2026portfolio,affinity2026portfolio}. Each presents
consolidated portfolio figures as a solved presentation problem. Kaplan and Lerner's account of
the underlying records cannot be wished away by a dashboard \citep{kaplan2016vcdata}. No vendor
page reports claim-level support accuracy or how conflicting sources are resolved.
The gap is not a missing screen. It is the absence of a tested rule for when a consolidated
figure may be said at all. A pipeline, a single grounded agent or a bought platform can each
present a pack. None of the reviewed sources shows that presentation survives a support check
when two eligible records disagree
\citep{buneman2001provenance,gao2023ragsurvey,guo2024multiagent}.
Section~\ref{sec:design-alternatives} and Table~\ref{tab:lit-solution-landscape} set those
presentation options beside the evidence contract.

\section{What makes a claim defensible}

Data quality is a property of a dataset in use, not of the dataset alone, so accuracy,
timeliness, completeness and consistency each need rules bound to a particular field and purpose
\citep{nikiforova2020quality,krasikov2020ready}. Provenance is the matching record of where a
statement came from and what was done to it. Foundational database work separates why a query
result exists from where its contributing values sat \citep{buneman2001provenance}. That explains
lineage, not whether a report is right. Documentation should record purpose, contents,
processing, gaps, errors, dependencies and corrections \citep{gebru2021datasheets}, and repeating
a result needs enough data, code, steps and measures \citep{pineau2021reproducibility}. A
defensible portfolio statement therefore links to its saved source, its exact place in that
source, the changes made to it, the checks applied and the approval given.

\subsection{Measure, repair or refuse: ALCE versus RARR}

Checking a source and checking a claim are two different steps. The source check covers access,
type, permission, date and whether a stable copy can be saved. The claim check asks whether an
exact passage supports the statement. Two studies by overlapping authors take different views of
what to do when that second check fails. The ALCE work treats attribution as a property to be measured, separating factual
correctness from citation precision and recall so that weak support becomes visible in the score
\citep{gao2023alce}. The RARR work treats it as a defect to be repaired, revising generated text
after the fact so that it matches retrieved evidence \citep{gao2023rarr}. Both are defensible,
and the choice is consequential: repair improves the finished text but relocates the decision
back inside the component that produced it. A third option, refusal, is to withhold claim
admission when the passage does not support the statement, and to record that outcome instead of
rewriting the text. Self-preference in model evaluators rises with self-recognition, which is a
demonstrated reason not to let the same component rewrite the claim it is judging
\citep{panickssery2024selfpref,pineau2021reproducibility}. Verification is also claim-level
rather than report-level. FActScore decomposes long-form text into atomic facts and scores the
share supported by a knowledge source, which is the grain at which admission can be refused
\citep{min2023factscore,gao2023alce}.

Where permission and technology allow, an accepted source should be saved as a fixed local copy
with a checksum, collection time, exact passage and version. A correction should create a new
version rather than replace the old one. Dataset and reproducibility guidance both support
recording external resources, updates, errors and superseded versions
\citep{gebru2021datasheets,pineau2021reproducibility}, and model cards apply the same logic to
versioned intended use and evaluation limits \citep{mitchell2019modelcards}. Some sources cannot
legally or technically be saved at all.

Conflicting evidence must be retained. Hallucination research distinguishes factual conflict,
unverifiable claims and overstatement, linking reliable output to uncertainty and checking
against trusted knowledge \citep{huang2023hallucination}. A source link does not resolve
disagreement when text conflicts with evidence or the source is wrong \citep{gao2023rarr}. One
citation marker cannot represent correctness, passage support and completeness
\citep{gao2023alce}. AttrScore finds that support is easier to judge than contradiction. The authors summarise GPT-4
at 81--83~per cent overall accuracy on AttrEval-GenSearch. The same local pages record overall
85.1 in the zero-shot setting and 84.3 in the few-shot setting, while contradiction remains the
weak class at 45.0 and 53.3 \citep{yue2023attrscore,huang2023hallucination}. Because contradiction
detection is weak, the ledger retains conflict for a named person rather than collapsing it in
software.
The system must not automatically prefer the majority, newest or most fluent version.

A traceable report therefore records the whole chain: discovery, checking, capture and use, then
the claim, support decision, conflict, approval and export. The design principle drawn from this
section is that a verifier should check evidence without being able to rewrite the claim,
precisely because correctness, support and attribution are distinct properties
\citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr,nist2023airmf,amershi2019guidelines}.
Figure~\ref{fig:lit-evidence-claim-chain}, in Appendix~\ref{app:supplementary-exhibits},
summarises this admission chain. Measure and repair are the two positions already tested in the
citation literature. Refusal of claim admission is the untested third, and it is the mechanism
RQ2 isolates \citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr}.

\section{Retrieval as remedy and vulnerability}

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) separates document indexing, retrieval and text generation,
and the literature holds two views of it that are hard to reconcile. In the hallucination
literature, retrieval is the remedy: grounding output in real documents reduces invented content
that a model would otherwise produce from memory \citep{huang2023hallucination}. In the security
literature, the same mechanism is the vulnerability, because indirect prompt injection lets text
hidden in retrieved pages alter a model's summary, queries, source choices or later tool use
\citep{greshake2023indirect}. Retrieval, in other words, fixes a problem of invention by opening
a channel for a problem of influence. Surveys of the field note that retrieval may equally miss
relevant material or return irrelevant passages \citep{gao2023ragsurvey}. The practical
consequence adopted here is that a large language model (LLM) may help find candidate sources,
while finding, checking, extracting and verifying stay separate steps that retrieved text cannot
reach into.

The saved Responses API documentation describes model-managed \texttt{web\_search}, inline
citations, annotations, domain filters and a list of consulted sources
\citep{openai2026websearch}. These features can help plan a search and produce a list of possible
sources \citep{gao2023ragsurvey}. They guarantee nothing about how many sources or citations come
back, and a visible citation may still be incomplete or unsupported \citep{gao2023alce}. Returned
URLs, snippets and model text stay possible sources until they pass the source checks.

Structured evidence should first be extracted with fixed, rule-based code. Unstructured public or
synthetic evidence may use an optional model, but only to suggest a claim that links to an exact
passage. Factual correctness, passage support and citation completeness remain different measures
\citep{gao2023alce}. A correct source link also does not prove a claim when the evidence
disagrees \citep{gao2023rarr}. Both routes end with fixed checks of the exact passage, data
structure and format, followed by separate verification. Unsupported content is rejected or left
unresolved. It is never filled in from model memory.

Public content found through search must be treated as untrusted data, not as instructions.
Indirect prompt injection can hide harmful instructions in searched material and change
summaries, queries, source choices or later actions \citep{greshake2023indirect}. RAG can also
receive noisy, conflicting or hostile text \citep{gao2023ragsurvey}. The prototype therefore
limits tool permissions, checks each destination, separates extraction from other actions, limits
redirects and requests, and stops safely when a check fails. Testing, uncertainty, separate
review and safe failure are recognised risk controls \citep{nist2023airmf}.
Figure~\ref{fig:lit-discovery-evidence-boundary}, in Appendix~\ref{app:literature-search},
separates finding a source from accepting it as evidence. A report should therefore state how much searching was permitted rather than imply that the web
was searched. Context size, token limits, domain filters and source lists are all configurable,
and the number of sources returned is not guaranteed \citep{openai2026websearch}, so a
repeatable method records the queries, sources, reporting date and budgets, then names what was
inaccessible, missing, conflicting or unverified
\citep{pineau2021reproducibility,gao2023alce}. Search may propose a source; fixed checks dispose
of it; retrieved text is data, never instruction
\citep{greshake2023indirect,nist2023airmf}.

\section{Who is allowed to decide}
\label{sec:design-alternatives}

Dividing a task between programmed roles is a design choice, not an acquisition of collective
intelligence. Reported systems differ in interface, role description and communication structure
\citep{guo2024multiagent}, while AutoGen shows that the developer, not the architecture, fixes
the roles, tools, control flow and stopping rules \citep{wu2024autogen}. This is why
Table~\ref{tab:lit-role-authority-matrix}, in Appendix~\ref{app:supplementary-exhibits}, assigns
each role one responsibility, defined inputs and outputs and a visible failure limit, and why
this section rests its case on authority rather than on role count
\citep{peffers2007dsrm,hevner2004design}.

One body of work argues that named roles help. Multi-agent debate can raise factuality on
reasoning benchmarks \citep{du2024debate,guo2024multiagent}. Reflexion separates an actor from an
evaluator and a reflection step, provided a reliable evaluation signal exists
\citep{shinn2023reflexion,wu2024autogen}. MetaGPT encodes standard operating procedures as role
hand-offs, and ChatDev adds explicit reviewer and tester roles in a chat-chain; both report gains
on software-engineering tasks \citep{hong2023metagpt,qian2024chatdev}. These results support
Position~A: role separation can help when the task supplies a checkable signal.

Position~B is the caution. Under matched inputs, at most one of six multi-agent systems in a 2026
preprint exceeded the single-agent anchor on a balanced accuracy average, while the remaining
five trailed by 2.56 to 11.29 points \citep{fu2026agents,cemri2025masfailures}. The same study is
the warrant for C1/C2 rather than an objection to this design: a comparison that does not hold
inputs equal cannot attribute a gain to extra agents. This pipeline is not claimed to beat a
single agent. Matched inputs are the only credible test, and the D0 comparison isolates a
recorded refusal of claim admission rather than a count of roles
\citep{fu2026agents,peffers2007dsrm}.

The clearest counterweight to any enthusiasm here is empirical. Analysis of 1,642 traces from
seven multi-agent systems identified fourteen failure modes spanning system design, inter-agent
alignment and task verification \citep{cemri2025masfailures}. That evidence deserves careful
reading rather than either dismissal or alarm: much of the wider dataset was annotated with
model assistance, and its coding and mathematics tasks differ from portfolio reporting. Read
cautiously, it still establishes that added roles are themselves a source of failure, which
makes programmed control flow, tool permissions and explicit stop rules necessary rather than
optional \citep{wu2024autogen,nist2023airmf}.

Human review gives a person authority to decide; it does not create new evidence. Here the
applied guidance and the experimental evidence are in genuine tension. The widely cited design
guidelines recommend making system limits clear, showing uncertainty and letting people dismiss
or correct output, treating transparency as the route to sound human judgement
\citep{amershi2019guidelines}. Bu\c{c}inca and colleagues tested something close to that
prescription and found it insufficient: participants still followed incorrect advice, and the
interventions that reduced over-reliance did so by making the task deliberately harder, with
uneven effects across participant groups \citep{bucinca2021forcing}. The implication is that the
remedy the guidelines propose is the one the experiment found wanting, so a well-designed review
screen cannot be assumed to produce better decisions. This project accordingly treats its
interface as an accountability mechanism rather than an accuracy mechanism. It shows the claim,
exact support, conflicts, uncertainty and content hash so that a named reviewer's decision is
recorded and attributable, and it leaves the question of whether review improves outcomes to a
study with participants \citep{nist2023airmf}.

Managed platforms make custom orchestration itself look optional, which is the sharpest
challenge to this project's design. AutoGen Studio already exposes models, tools, memory and
workflows through a visual interface, so much of the wiring described above can be bought rather
than written; its authors nonetheless call the tool not production-ready and name architecture
comparison and reliability as open problems \citep{dibia2024autogenstudio}. Vendor documentation
for Grok Bot goes further, describing persistent named Bots with distinct jobs, tools, working
rules and approval boundaries \citep{spacexai2026grokbot}. The unresolved question is not
whether a platform can host roles, but whether it can enforce an evidence contract. Convenience
in configuring agents is a different property from correctness in admitting claims, and neither
source measures the second \citep{dibia2024autogenstudio,guo2024multiagent}. Evaluating such a workflow therefore means keeping its failures visible rather than reporting
only its successes. Logs should carry role, task, attempt, input, output, hash, time, tokens,
errors and recovery, and fixed cases should cover each role and the whole workflow, including
disagreement and safe failure
\citep{nist2023airmf,pineau2021reproducibility,guo2024multiagent}. The benefit of role
separation is therefore contested; matched inputs are the test; this pipeline is not claimed to
beat a single agent \citep{fu2026agents,cemri2025masfailures}.

\section{Settled / contested / gap}

Three findings are settled enough to bind the design. A reporting claim needs a legal identity,
a reporting date and a saved source; citation is not the same as support; and conflict should
be retained rather than averaged
\citep{buneman2001provenance,gao2023alce,kaplan2016vcdata}. Two findings
remain contested: whether extra programmed roles improve outcomes under matched inputs, and
whether human review improves decisions rather than merely recording them
\citep{fu2026agents,amershi2019guidelines,bucinca2021forcing}. The gap this dissertation
addresses is narrower than a missing product. No located study tests whether a separately
recorded verification stage changes what a report may say when inputs are held equal, or
whether evidence survives from intake to approved export. Fu and colleagues' matched-input
result is why a manual baseline is postponed to the pilot in
Section~\ref{sec:business-pilot} rather than treated as an omitted control
\citep{fu2026agents,peffers2007dsrm}.

For RQ1, the established problem is that company and funding records can be incomplete, delayed
and hard to link, while an exact citation can still sit beside a wrong or partial statement
\citep{thorne2026funding,kaplan2016vcdata,gao2023rarr}. What remains unresolved is what survives
the whole chain. RQ1 therefore needs labelled outcomes at each step, not a demonstration that a
report can be produced \citep{nikiforova2020quality,pineau2021reproducibility}. For RQ2, splitting
the work between named roles can make responsibilities visible, but surveys and trace analysis
also find failures of coordination, verification and system design; one program applying the
same fixed gate remains a credible alternative \citep{guo2024multiagent,cemri2025masfailures}.
What remains unresolved is whether a separately recorded verifier changes which claims are
admitted when the inputs are equal. Table~\ref{tab:lit-settled-contested-gap} maps those
positions to the questions Chapter~3 will operationalise. How that instrument is built, and why
a designed suite is used instead of a field rate, is a methods question rather than a literature
finding \citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}.
