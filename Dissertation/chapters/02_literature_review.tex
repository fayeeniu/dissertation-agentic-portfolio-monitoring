\chapter{Literature Review}
\section{Scope and structure of the literature review}

The literature relevant to this dissertation spans several connected fields rather than one established
research stream. Studies of early-stage finance examine how funding rounds, public support and investor
activity may signal company development, but their findings depend on the platform, sample and reporting
context \citep{estrin2024digital,wasti2024successive}. Research on company registers also shows that
public records can support analysis while remaining incomplete, delayed or difficult to link across
sources \citep{hardman2023small,thorne2026funding}. These findings make legal identity, reporting date
and source context central to reliable portfolio reporting.

A second body of work considers how raw information becomes a defensible report, treating data quality
as dependent on intended use, requiring that origins and assumptions be documented, and separating
factual correctness from citation support
\citep{nikiforova2020quality,gebru2021datasheets,gao2023alce}. A third concerns AI-assisted retrieval,
multi-agent workflows and human control, where each proposed remedy carries a matching risk:
retrieval can return irrelevant or hostile material, extra agent roles add their own failures, and
human review may not correct what it is shown
\citep{gao2023ragsurvey,guo2024multiagent,bucinca2021forcing}. The sections that follow work through
these themes in turn, and the chapter closes by identifying the gap this dissertation addresses.

\section{Early-stage finance, portfolio reporting, and public company intelligence}

Founders usually know more about an early-stage business than outside investors, and funding platforms
shape what information is visible. Two studies illustrate how differently that visibility can be
read. Estrin and colleagues work across several countries and find that amounts raised and investor
participation vary by pitch, sector, region, country and platform, but their sample contains
successful pitches rather than every attempt \citep{estrin2024digital}. Wasti and colleagues go the
other way, studying repeat funding in depth within a single platform and taking the campaign round as
the unit of analysis \citep{wasti2024successive}. The two are not in conflict about facts, yet they
pull in opposite directions on what a funding event means: breadth across platforms buys
generalisability at the cost of a censored sample, while depth within one platform buys a clean unit at
the cost of transferability. Neither design can license a general inference from investor numbers to
company quality, which is why this project treats a funding round as a dated, sourced event rather
than a signal to be scored.

Portfolio reports should record funding and public support as dated events with clear sources. In the
Crowdcube study, an earlier successful round may act as a signal, but its meaning still depends on the
campaign and platform \citep{wasti2024successive}. UK research on public support found later differences
between participating firms, but it also noted selection effects and did not claim that the support
caused those differences \citep{mondal2025state}. Rebuilding the UK funding process requires links
between opportunities, applications, decisions, projects, organisations and outcomes, even though
these records often lack shared identifiers \citep{thorne2026funding}. A round, grant or award can
support a dated fact. It cannot prove future success or provide a complete funding history.

These events need to be linked to the correct legal company, and the literature on the UK register
disagrees productively about how much that register can carry. Galanakis and colleagues treat
incorporation records as a timely and broad indicator of company creation, valuing the register
precisely for its currency \citep{galanakis2026chrt}. Hardman reaches an almost opposite conclusion
about recent data, showing that filing rules and delays make the newest records the least complete
part of the register \citep{hardman2023small}. Krasikov and colleagues then add a third position: open
access is not the same as readiness, because a register may be fully published and still unsuitable
for a given business task \citep{krasikov2020ready}. The disagreement is real but resolvable, and the
resolution is that timeliness holds for the fact of incorporation and fails for financial filings.
This project therefore accepts register identity as authoritative while treating recency of financial
records as a property to be checked per field rather than assumed.

Two constraints follow for what may be reported. Public-support differences may reflect which firms
applied rather than any effect of the support \citep{mondal2025state}, and financial communications
must present benefits and risks fairly \citep{fca2024promotions}. Together these rule out causal
performance claims, speculative valuations and any general company-quality score.

\subsection{Sector importance and existing reporting approaches}

The reporting problem sits within a material but uneven early-stage investment market. The British
Business Bank records \pounds10.8 billion of announced UK smaller-business equity investment across
2,048 deals in 2024, while deal numbers fell by 15.1 per cent and seed- and venture-stage activity
weakened \citep{britishbusinessbank2025equity}. Monitoring also consumes professional attention: a
survey of 885 institutional venture capitalists reported an average of 18 hours each week working
with portfolio companies and about 60 per cent interacting with them at least weekly
\citep{gompers2016vcdecisions}. These descriptive results come from different periods and settings;
monitoring is broader than reporting, and neither source measures the effect of a reporting tool.

Private-market evidence also limits what reporting can consolidate. The British Business Bank treats
angel-investment evidence as partial because much activity is private and reporting is patchy
\citep{britishbusinessbank2025equity}, and register research reaches a parallel conclusion about
public records being published yet unfit for a given task \citep{krasikov2020ready}. Portfolio
managers, analysts and downstream report users therefore need dates, definitions, legal identities and
visible coverage gaps. Taken together, these sources establish the reporting problem as one of
traceability under incomplete evidence, which is a different problem from the efficiency case usually
made for automation.

Several technical approaches are plausible. Manual documents and spreadsheets preserve flexible
judgement, but their provenance and consistency depend on the local practice. Deterministic data
pipelines can apply repeatable identity, time and quality rules, although lineage must be explicit and
schema changes can invalidate those rules \citep{buneman2001provenance,nikiforova2020quality}.
Retrieval-augmented generation can extend unstructured search but may retrieve irrelevant or hostile
material, while one fixed process or several bounded roles can apply the same final support rule
\citep{gao2023ragsurvey,guo2024multiagent}. The reviewed sources do not measure this project's manual
workflow, so effort and error cannot be assigned to any approach before a matched study.

Commercial products cover related but non-equivalent workflows, and setting them beside the
data-quality literature exposes an unresolved tension at the centre of this field. Visible describes
portfolio-data collection, monitoring, dashboards and investor reporting; Zapflow describes integrated
deal and portfolio records; and Affinity describes relationship-centred activity capture and reports
\citep{visible2026investors,zapflow2026portfolio,affinity2026portfolio}. Each presents consolidated
portfolio figures as a solved presentation problem. Yet Kaplan and Lerner describe the underlying
private-market data as incomplete and inconsistent, with definitional differences, missing failed
firms and backfilling \citep{kaplan2016vcdata}. Both cannot be straightforwardly true: a dashboard
cannot be more reliable than the records beneath it, and no vendor page reports claim-level support
accuracy or how conflicting sources are resolved. That gap between confident presentation and
acknowledged data weakness is the space this dissertation works in.

than scoring products.

\section{Data quality, company identity, missing data and time}

Data quality is a property of a dataset in use, not of the dataset alone, so accuracy, timeliness,
completeness and consistency each need rules bound to a particular field and purpose
\citep{nikiforova2020quality,krasikov2020ready}. The practical consequence is that a portfolio report
must state how each field will be used and which checks matter for it, because a single combined
quality score can conceal a serious fault in one field.

Company matching also depends on the source. An exact company number from the official register is the
main legal identifier; a company name is only a possible match. Funding sources without shared
identifiers need careful exact or approximate matching, and errors and gaps still remain
\citep{thorne2026funding}. Work across several registers also required name normalisation, approximate
comparison and manual checks when company or country names disagreed \citep{surak2026gateways}.
Companies House identifies incorporated companies rather than all economic activity
\citep{galanakis2026chrt}. An uncertain match should therefore stay unresolved until a named reviewer
checks it. Records with similar names must not be merged automatically.

Missing data must keep its meaning instead of becoming a default number. Documentation should state
what is missing, why it is missing and what is still unknown \citep{gebru2021datasheets}. A financial
table contains values, empty cells, positions and different types of information. An empty cell does
not automatically mean zero \citep{bradley2024synfintabs}. Completeness and validity are also separate
qualities that need their own checks \citep{nikiforova2020quality}. This project distinguishes a blank,
zero, none, not applicable, not reported, not found publicly, invalid and observed value. Not found
publicly does not mean that something does not exist. These categories are a project design choice,
not a general standard from the literature.

The system also needs separate dates. It records when an event happened, when the information became
available, when it was collected and the last date allowed in the report. Register snapshots and
filing delays show why recent facts may not have been available at an earlier reporting date
\citep{hardman2023small}. Incorporation and later business activity also happen at different times
\citep{galanakis2026chrt}. Using information published after the reporting date creates data leakage
and may make an evaluation look better than it should \citep{kapoor2023leakage}. A correction needs a
new version and date. It must not be used to support an earlier report.

Together these rules give a testable evidence model, recording company identity, the type of missing
value, event and availability dates, source, exact passage and version, and keeping invalid,
conflicting and unresolved records visible. Its checks are tied to the task and the data
\citep{nikiforova2020quality,gebru2021datasheets}, and its dating rules exist to keep later
information out of an earlier report \citep{kapoor2023leakage}. Chapter 3 puts this model to work.

\section{Source records, source checks, conflicts and traceable reporting}

Provenance is the record of where a statement came from and what was done to it. Foundational
database work separates why a query result exists from where its contributing values sat
\citep{buneman2001provenance}; that explains lineage, not whether a report is right. Documentation
should record purpose, contents, processing, gaps, errors, dependencies and corrections
\citep{gebru2021datasheets}, and repeating a result needs enough data, code, steps and measures
\citep{pineau2021reproducibility}. Each portfolio statement should therefore link to its saved
source, its exact place in that source, the changes made to it, the checks applied and the approval
given.

Checking a source and checking a claim are two different steps: the source check covers access, type,
permission, date and whether a stable copy can be saved, while the claim check asks whether an exact
passage supports the statement. Two studies by overlapping authors take notably different views of
what to do when that second check fails. The ALCE work treats attribution as a property to be measured,
separating factual correctness from citation precision and recall so that weak support becomes
visible in the score \citep{gao2023alce}. The RARR work treats it instead as a defect to be repaired,
revising generated text after the fact so that it matches retrieved evidence \citep{gao2023rarr}. Both
are defensible, and the choice between them is consequential rather than technical: repair improves
the finished text but relocates the decision back inside the component that produced it. This project
follows the measurement position and refuses repair, holding an unsupported claim rather than editing
it into compliance, with the decision and files recorded so the outcome can be repeated
\citep{pineau2021reproducibility}.

Where permission and technology allow, an accepted source should be saved as a fixed local copy with a
checksum, collection time, exact passage and version, and a correction should create a new version
rather than replace the old one. Dataset and reproducibility guidance both support recording external
resources, updates, errors and superseded versions
\citep{gebru2021datasheets,pineau2021reproducibility}, and model cards apply the same logic to
versioned intended use and evaluation limits \citep{mitchell2019modelcards}. Some sources, of course,
cannot legally or technically be saved at all.

Conflicting evidence must be retained. Hallucination research distinguishes factual conflict,
unverifiable claims and overstatement, linking reliable output to uncertainty and checking against
trusted knowledge \citep{huang2023hallucination}. A source link does not resolve disagreement when text
conflicts with evidence or the source is wrong \citep{gao2023rarr}; nor can one citation marker represent
correctness, passage support and completeness \citep{gao2023alce}. Supported disagreements remain grouped
and unverifiable claims unresolved until a stated rule or named reviewer decides them. The system must
not automatically prefer the majority, newest or most fluent version.

A traceable report therefore records the whole chain: discovery, checking, capture and use, then the
claim, support decision, conflict, approval and export. The design principle drawn from this section is
that a verifier should check evidence without being able to rewrite the claim, precisely because
correctness, support and attribution are distinct properties
\citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr,nist2023airmf,amershi2019guidelines}.

\section{LLM-assisted source finding and evidence extraction}

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) separates document indexing, retrieval and text generation, and
the literature holds two views of it that are hard to reconcile. In the hallucination literature,
retrieval is the remedy: grounding output in real documents reduces invented content that a model
would otherwise produce from memory \citep{huang2023hallucination}. In the security literature, the
same mechanism is the vulnerability, because indirect prompt injection lets text hidden in retrieved
pages alter a model's summary, queries, source choices or subsequent tool use
\citep{greshake2023indirect}. Retrieval, in other words, fixes a problem of invention by opening a
channel for a problem of influence, and surveys of the field note that retrieval may equally miss
relevant material or return irrelevant passages \citep{gao2023ragsurvey}. The practical consequence
adopted here is that a large language model (LLM) may help find candidate sources, while finding,
checking, extracting and verifying stay separate steps that retrieved text cannot reach into.

The saved Responses API documentation describes model-managed \texttt{web\_search}, inline citations,
annotations, domain filters and a list of consulted sources \citep{openai2026websearch}. These features
can help plan a search and produce a list of possible sources \citep{gao2023ragsurvey}. They
guarantee nothing about how many sources or citations come back, though, and a visible citation may
still be incomplete or unsupported \citep{gao2023alce}. Returned URLs, snippets and model text stay
possible sources until they pass the source checks.

Structured evidence should first be extracted with fixed, rule-based code. Unstructured public or
synthetic evidence may use an optional model, but only to suggest a claim that links to an exact
passage. Factual correctness, passage support and citation completeness remain different measures
\citep{gao2023alce}. A correct source link also does not prove a claim when the evidence disagrees
\citep{gao2023rarr}. Both routes end with fixed checks of the exact passage, data structure and format,
followed by separate verification. This reflects the need to test relevance, support, rejection and
reliability separately \citep{gao2023ragsurvey}. Unsupported content is rejected or left unresolved.
It is never filled in from model memory.

Public content found through search must be treated as untrusted data, not as instructions. Indirect
prompt injection can hide harmful instructions in searched material and change summaries, queries,
source choices or later actions \citep{greshake2023indirect}. RAG can also receive noisy, conflicting
or hostile text \citep{gao2023ragsurvey}. The prototype therefore limits tool permissions, checks each
destination, separates extraction from other actions, limits redirects and requests, and stops safely
when a check fails. Testing, uncertainty, separate review and safe failure are recognised risk controls
\citep{nist2023airmf}.

A report should therefore state how much searching was permitted rather than imply that the web was
searched. Context size, token limits, domain filters and source lists are all configurable, and the
number of sources returned is not guaranteed \citep{openai2026websearch}, so a repeatable method
records the queries, sources, reporting date and budgets, then names what was inaccessible, missing,
conflicting or unverified \citep{pineau2021reproducibility,gao2023alce}.

\section{Functional role separation and human approval}

Dividing a task between programmed roles is a design choice, not an acquisition of collective
intelligence, and the literature is strikingly inconsistent about what such a division even is:
reported systems differ in interface, role description and communication structure
\citep{guo2024multiagent}, while AutoGen shows that the developer, not the architecture, fixes the
roles, tools, control flow and stopping rules \citep{wu2024autogen}. This is why
Table~\ref{tab:lit-role-authority-matrix} assigns each role one responsibility, defined inputs and
outputs and a visible failure limit, and why Section~\ref{sec:design-alternatives} rests its case on
authority rather than on role count \citep{peffers2007dsrm}.

The clearest counterweight to any enthusiasm here is empirical. Analysis of 1,642 traces from seven
multi-agent systems identified fourteen failure modes spanning system design, inter-agent alignment
and task verification \citep{cemri2025masfailures}. That evidence deserves careful reading rather than
either dismissal or alarm: much of the wider dataset was annotated with model assistance, and its
coding and mathematics tasks differ from portfolio reporting. Read cautiously, it still establishes
that added roles are themselves a source of failure, which makes programmed control flow, tool
permissions and explicit stop rules necessary rather than optional
\citep{wu2024autogen,nist2023airmf}.

Human review gives a person authority to decide; it does not create new evidence. Here the applied
guidance and the experimental evidence are in genuine tension. The widely cited design guidelines
recommend making system limits clear, showing uncertainty and letting people dismiss or correct output,
treating transparency as the route to sound human judgement \citep{amershi2019guidelines}. Bu\c{c}inca
and colleagues tested something close to that prescription and found it insufficient: participants
still followed incorrect advice, and the interventions that reduced over-reliance did so by making the
task deliberately harder, with uneven effects across participant groups \citep{bucinca2021forcing}. The
uncomfortable implication is that the remedy the guidelines propose is the one the experiment found
wanting, so a well-designed review screen cannot be assumed to produce better decisions. This project
accordingly treats its interface as an accountability mechanism rather than an accuracy mechanism: it
shows the claim, exact support, conflicts, uncertainty and content hash so that a named reviewer's
decision is recorded and attributable, and it leaves the question of whether review improves outcomes
to a study with participants \citep{nist2023airmf}.

Evaluating such a workflow therefore means keeping its failures visible rather than reporting only its
successes. Logs should carry role, task, attempt, input, output, hash, time, tokens, errors and
recovery, and fixed cases should cover each role and the whole workflow, including disagreement and
safe failure \citep{nist2023airmf,pineau2021reproducibility,guo2024multiagent}.

Managed platforms make custom orchestration itself look optional, which is the sharpest challenge to
this project's design. AutoGen Studio already exposes models, tools, memory and workflows through a
visual interface, so much of the wiring described above can be bought rather than written; its authors
nonetheless call the tool not production-ready and name architecture comparison and reliability as
open problems \citep{dibia2024autogenstudio}. Vendor documentation for Grok Bot goes further,
describing persistent named Bots with distinct jobs, tools, working rules and approval boundaries
\citep{spacexai2026grokbot}. The unresolved question is not whether a platform can host roles, but
whether it can enforce an evidence contract; convenience in configuring agents is a different property
from correctness in admitting claims, and neither source measures the second
\citep{dibia2024autogenstudio,guo2024multiagent}.

\section{Main findings from the literature and research gap}

No single best reporting design emerges from this literature, and the disagreements traced above
explain why. Private-market datasets have limits of coverage and definition, work on lineage and
citation keeps a statement's origin apart from its support and its truth, and vendor pages offer no
independent comparison
\citep{kaplan2016vcdata,buneman2001provenance,gao2023alce,visible2026investors}. What is missing is
therefore evidence rather than novelty: whether rules for identity, missing values, time, source
records, verification and human authority hold together on the same early-stage inputs.

For RQ1, the established problem is that company and funding records can be incomplete, delayed and
hard to link, while an exact citation can still sit beside a wrong or partial statement
\citep{thorne2026funding,kaplan2016vcdata,gao2023rarr}. What remains unresolved is what survives the
whole chain: whether fixed rules for identity, time, missing values and source support carry the
intended meaning through, and hold back a claim they cannot support. RQ1 therefore needs labelled
outcomes at each step, claim support, complete source records and agreement across repeats, not a
demonstration that a report can be produced
\citep{nikiforova2020quality,pineau2021reproducibility}.

For RQ2, splitting the work between named roles can make responsibilities visible, but surveys and
trace analysis also find failures of coordination, verification and system design; one program
applying the same fixed gate remains a credible alternative
\citep{guo2024multiagent,cemri2025masfailures}. What remains unresolved is whether a separately
recorded verifier changes which claims are admitted when the inputs are equal, and at what cost. RQ2 therefore compares conditions with and without that verifier using supported-claim precision,
recall, unsupported-claim rate, abstention, failures, time, tokens and cost
\citep{peffers2007dsrm,pineau2021reproducibility}. A favourable result cannot be attributed to agent
labels without a comparison that isolates the rule and separation.

This choice of evaluation instrument needs its own justification, because a small set of designed
cases is easily mistaken for a weak substitute for a large sample. Ribeiro and colleagues argue the
opposite for behavioural testing: held-out accuracy on a single test set overstates real capability,
and a structured suite of targeted cases, each built to probe one specific behaviour, exposes failures
that aggregate scores conceal \citep{ribeiro2020checklist}. That is the warrant for the D0 design in
Chapter~3. It also fixes what such a suite can conclude, and here it sits usefully against Kapoor and
Narayanan's account of over-optimistic machine-learning evaluation, which shows how easily designed or
leaky setups flatter a system \citep{kapoor2023leakage}. Read together, the two support a specific and
limited position: a behavioural suite is diagnostic rather than estimative, so passing it demonstrates
that a targeted control is present and cannot establish a rate on unseen data.

For future human evaluation, guidance supports correction and decision authority, while controlled
evidence also shows usability and over-reliance trade-offs
\citep{amershi2019guidelines,bucinca2021forcing}. Commercial pages describe dashboards and reporting
workflows, but not independently measured reviewer effort or decision quality
\citep{visible2026investors,zapflow2026portfolio}. Whether named review changes correctness,
completeness, editing effort or elapsed time therefore remains a pilot question. An implemented
approval screen cannot answer it.

Public-web research remains a separate engineering case, because retrieved content carries both
prompt-injection and weak-support risks \citep{greshake2023indirect,gao2023alce}, and
Figure~\ref{fig:lit-discovery-evidence-boundary} separates finding a source from accepting it as
evidence. The live comparison between official sources alone and LLM-assisted search was never fixed
before evaluation, so it is treated as neither a question nor a result
\citep{openai2026websearch,pineau2021reproducibility}. Chapter 3 turns these gaps into the fixed
evaluation method.
