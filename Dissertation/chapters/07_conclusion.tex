\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
\section{Answers to the primary question, RQ1 and RQ2}

The primary question is answered within the controlled scope. The implemented workflow can preserve
identity, reporting-period, missing-state, provenance, verification and approval records from intake
to report export. Engineering checks and repository artefacts establish that these boundaries exist
in the evaluated local version \citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}. The dissertation's conclusion
is therefore narrower and stronger than the original research contract: it demonstrates an
evidence-first implementation and one controlled verification effect. Manual comparison, participant
review, live source use and business benefit are outside the active questions because the required
observations were unavailable \citep{hevner2004design,pineau2021reproducibility}.

For RQ1, the strongest result is traceable execution. D0 decisions can be followed from a fictional
candidate and its evidence to the verifier state and report consequence, including the observed-zero
that was admitted and the grant contradiction that was refused. The complete outcome list was
identical across three repeats \citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr,pineau2021reproducibility}.

For RQ2, separate verification changed claim admission under equal inputs. C1 emitted six candidates
that D0 labelled unsupported, while C2 refused all six and retained the five supported claims.
Precision changed from 0.455 to 1.000, the unsupported-claim rate from 0.545 to 0.000, and recall
remained 1.000. The mechanism is refusal of claim admission, not discovery of additional evidence
\citep{gao2023alce,fu2026agents}.

Three binding limits apply to every sentence above. D0 cases were written beside the rules they test.
No authorised observations exist for a manual baseline, named review, live sources or a managed
platform. The prototype is local and single-user. Section~\ref{sec:limitations} states those limits
in full \citep{ribeiro2020checklist,kapoor2023leakage}.

\section{Contribution}

The main contribution is a reporting workflow that makes source admission, claim support, conflicts,
missing information and release authority explicit. Separating the roles gives production,
verification and approval different permissions and saved hand-offs
\citep{buneman2001provenance,pineau2021reproducibility}. The design is inspectable because the saved
records link each decision to a source, rule, version and output. That inspectability is the property
defended here, not a measured gain in accuracy on unseen companies
\citep{gao2023alce,hevner2004design}. The literature contribution is the same point restated as a
criterion: who is allowed to decide, and under what recorded refusal, is a design question that
has to be answered before extra roles are counted as a result
\citep{fu2026agents,peffers2007dsrm}.

The empirical contribution is the paired D0 comparison. It reports aggregate metrics and two
end-to-end claims, including a correct accepted zero and a refused contradiction. The result supports
the verification contract on the fixture rather than a new algorithm or a claim of production
performance \citep{gao2023alce,gao2023rarr}.

The practical contribution is an executable pilot design with six named parts: integration, staff
responsibilities, cost model, time-saving hypotheses, go/no-go metrics, and testing still required
\citep{peffers2007dsrm,nist2023airmf}. Time saving and cost reduction remain hypotheses until that
pilot is authorised and run. The contribution is inspectability of admission, not a measured gain in
analyst hours \citep{gao2023alce,peffers2007dsrm}.

\section{Sequenced future evidence}

The first next step is the prospective business pilot in Section~\ref{sec:business-pilot}. It should
freeze the data, source window, reference decisions, completion rule and acceptance criteria before
collecting paired manual and workflow observations \citep{kapoor2023leakage,pineau2021reproducibility}.

A separate live-source study can then compare approved official connectors with bounded assisted
discovery using identical companies, dates, source limits and output requirements. The outstanding
testing listed in Section~\ref{sec:business-pilot} must be finished before remote or multi-user
operation is considered \citep{nist2023airmf,cddo2023genai}.

A managed platform should then be tested against the custom orchestration layer. The question is
whether a bought platform can reproduce the same evidence-admission, verification and approval
contract for less setup and maintenance effort, measured under equivalent inputs
\citep{spacexai2026grokbot,fu2026agents,pineau2021reproducibility}.

The full evidence roadmap is Table~\ref{tab:conc-evidence-roadmap} in
Appendix~\ref{app:supplementary-exhibits}. Each future stage requires an explicit authority and stop
decision. None is presented as a result of this dissertation
\citep{nist2023airmf,pineau2021reproducibility}.

The sequence is also a refusal to treat the present 1.000 as a standing invitation to scale. A
matched-input comparison that already isolates refusal of claim admission should be the first
authorised study, not a wider panel of the same designed cases \citep{fu2026agents,ribeiro2020checklist}.
Independent labels, a sealed freeze and a named approval owner are prerequisites, not optional
polish. Until they exist, the honest report is the one that still points to
Table~\ref{tab:meth-evidence-scope} \citep{kapoor2023leakage,pineau2021reproducibility}.

\section{Closing statement}

Early-stage portfolio reporting fails, when it fails, as an evidence problem rather than as a writing
problem. This dissertation built a local workflow that refuses to admit a claim the saved passage
does not support, and it showed that a separately recorded refusal changes what a designed report
may say when the inputs are held equal. The next honest step is not a larger score on the same
fixture. It is a matched pilot in which a named team, a frozen source window and a manual baseline
are in place before anyone reads the output \citep{hevner2004design,peffers2007dsrm}. Until that
evidence exists, the defensible sentence remains the one that can still point to its source. A
fluent paragraph that cannot do so is not an improvement on a silence, and a perfect score on a
fixture written beside its own rules is not a reason to forget that distinction
\citep{thorat2024summexecedit,ribeiro2020checklist}. The report therefore ends on a sequenced next
step rather than on a larger claim: freeze the inputs, run the matched pilot, and only then ask
whether the same refusal still changes what a real pack may say
\citep{peffers2007dsrm,kapoor2023leakage}.
